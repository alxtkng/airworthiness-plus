# Contract Documents: Drafts

Step 8 of private beta. Date: 19 November 2026.

Three drafts for future customers. **None is legal advice and none is ready to sign.** They were written by the build session to set out what the product can honestly promise today, so that a solicitor has something accurate to turn into contract terms. Every reference to legislation is from memory and is To Be Checked.

| Document | What It Is For |
|---|---|
| `data-processing-agreement-draft.md` | The terms on which the company processes personal data for an organisation |
| `service-level-draft.md` | What the service promises on availability, recovery and support |
| `data-export-on-exit.md` | What an organisation receives when it leaves, and what happens to its data afterwards |

Before any is offered to a customer: review by a solicitor; the company's details filled in; the hosting and backup providers chosen; the figures marked Proposed confirmed by Alex King.
