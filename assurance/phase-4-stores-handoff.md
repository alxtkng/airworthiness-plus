## Receipt to stock contract

Goods In supplies the organisation, immutable final disposition UUID and receipt UUID, whole-line outcome and source dates. The proposed posting input is the disposition UUID within the current organisation. Stores reads the persisted final decision and evidence links. Submitted quantity, outcome or approval data cannot override those records. Goods In records the delivered measure and identity at receipt and the verified evidence and physical destination at disposition. Stores owns the location register, compatibility checks and authorised legacy mappings. The receipt keeps the delivery line reference, item and class, supplier or owner source, original arrival document and intended use. The disposition keeps the inspector, authority, maintenance approval and dated reference, inspection and physical movement dates, reason, and the evidence actually relied on. Preserve the operative check IDs, including the shelf, identity and condition observations, and their date and entry-order precedence. Acceptance applies to one line and one outcome. Use distinct delivery-line references for split outcomes, different lots or multiple serials, with a shared supplier delivery reference where applicable.

| Input | New lines | Existing lines and reconciliation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quantity and unit | Proposed initial units are each and litre. Use exact decimals with up to three fractional places for litres. Require whole quantities for each and exactly one each for a serial. Propose a per-line ceiling of 2,147,483,647 to accommodate the full existing positive integer range. Refuse quantities outside the supported scale or range. | Carry the existing integer exactly when independent evidence confirms the unit and quantity. Where pack size or the delivered measure is ambiguous, hold for review. Require evidence for the unit before posting. |
| Traceable identity | Record separate part number, serial, batch or lot, and standard/specification as applicable. Components require a part number and serial where serialised, or documented lot or other trace where unnumbered. Standard parts require the applicable standard and trace to the received lot or package. Materials require specification and batch or evidenced equivalent trace. Use manufacturer and part identity to disambiguate serials. | Keep the source text. An authorised person records the structured mapping and evidence in a separate dated record. Missing or ambiguous identity blocks serviceable posting. |
| Destination | Use a stable same-organisation location ID for Stores and quarantine, with the physical destination and its rules captured at posting. Serviceable locations accept only allowed classes and storage conditions. Quarantine requires a secure compatible location, a tag and a reason. Returns retain a recipient and return evidence. | Map a free-text destination only from dated, retrievable evidence that identifies the actual location. An unresolved quarantine remains an active visible hold with physical location to verify and no availability. |
| Inspection and provenance | Require current applicable passing checks and a retrievable reference tied to the actual item. For serviceable components, require a documented release or the exact applicable equivalent or permitted exception and its basis. For standard parts, require conformity to the applicable standard and traceability. For materials, require conformity to the specification, manufacturing source and supplier source. Capture manufacturer storage and expiry basis, or evidenced inapplicability. | Record a dated evidence assessment against the original records. If original acceptance cannot be substantiated, keep it blocked and require fresh inspection through Goods In where applicable. Preserve all original documents, decisions and dates. |

Proposed implementation decisions for owner review. Goods In captures delivered quantity, unit and the as-received identifiers. At disposition the inspector verifies those identifiers, records the applicable evidence basis and selects the physical location from the organisation’s active Stores or quarantine locations. Stores maintains those locations and checks their compatibility again at posting. For a supplier return, Goods In records the recipient and return evidence. A dated legacy mapping may resolve terminology or missing structured values when the original evidence substantiates the decision and the item. A missing or contradictory inspection basis keeps the acceptance blocked and goes back to Goods In for a fresh linked assessment where the organisation’s procedure permits it. Preserve the original receipt, immutable disposition and `StockEntry` in either case. Use the same disposition-keyed posting transaction for a substantiated legacy acceptance and a new acceptance. Apply [ADR-0002](../architecture/decisions/ADR-0002-data-layer.md) to the added records, including same-organisation parent checks, forced row-level security and database-written history.

| Final result | PR 1 outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting Inspection | No final disposition, stock posting or stock balance. |
| Accepted into Stores with all data resolved | Post one intake movement and credit the full exact quantity in its unit in a compatible serviceable location. An accepted line with uncertain applicable shelf life remains awaiting posting. A line valid on its evidenced arrival date can be reconciled after expiry, with current availability zero. |
| Accepted into Stores with unresolved data | Show **Awaiting Stock Posting**, list every blocking reason, retain all source links and credit zero until resolution. |
| Quarantined | Create a visible blocked holding linked to the disposition. Show verified blocked custody when its location and quantity are known. Keep a visible hold and show the missing details when either is unresolved. Available quantity is always zero. |
| Returned to Supplier | Show the original disposition in return history. Credit zero. |

Use one database transaction for the posting journal and its initial movement or verified holding. A database uniqueness constraint on the disposition link, ADR-0002 tenant guards and row locks enforce one posting and prevent parallel callers crediting twice. The same request returns the original posted record. Conflicting retries preserve it and surface a conflict. A transaction failure leaves no opening movement or partial credit. Reconciliation is restartable through that same path and preserves the original physical dates as well as the actual posting date and actor. Corrections append evidence. A later movement has its own ID, source and destination and preserves quantities in the same unit. Lock the source disposition before posting and affected stock positions in stable ID order before moving. Agree the package-before-stock lock order with Planning before PR 2 changes release or issue. A serial is indivisible and has one current custody position.

One register serves each organisation. On hand measures physical custody by item and location. Reserved is the amount allocated to work. Available is the unreserved amount in serviceable custody that passes current stock-level checks. Specific approval and work-package eligibility are determined at issue. PR 1 has no reservation workflow, so its reserved balance starts at zero. Initial acceptance supplies evidence for the approval under which the inspector accepted the item. An additional approval requires separate dated evidence tying the same lot or serial, condition, release or evidence basis, scope, actor and authority to that approval. PR 2 checks the package’s pinned approval and checks condition, shelf life and eligibility again at use or fitting. A maintenance dispatch and subsequent receipt keep the original identity and custody trail. PR 3 requires a fresh linked Goods In inspection and acceptance of the returned item, based on the maintenance release and applicable checks, before serviceable re-entry. That fresh disposition uses the same once-only posting rule and transfers existing blocked custody without increasing the total held. A terminal scrap identity cannot re-enter.
