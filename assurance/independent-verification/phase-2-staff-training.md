# Independent review of Staff recurrent training

27 November 2026. A separate reviewer inspected the Staff training form, retained evidence, Staff summary, migration, role checks and sample seed.

| Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|
| The UTC date can reject a completion dated today just after UK midnight during British Summer Time | Use the application’s UK date helper |
| The Staff summary omits the completion date and leaves a missing renewal date ambiguous | Display the completion date and identify missing renewal dates |
| Duplicate detection, grouping and database uniqueness use different rules to compare subjects | Use the database’s lowercase comparison for all three |

Each earlier completion remains visible on the person record. The sample completion appears when a database is freshly seeded. The wider Phase 2 review remains open.
