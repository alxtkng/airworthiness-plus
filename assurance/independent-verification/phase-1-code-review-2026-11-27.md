# Independent Review of Phase 1 of the Canvas Build

Phase 1 of `prompts/canvas-build.md`, the code merged between commits 7830453 and 1394296 (pull requests 121 to 139), was reviewed on 27 November 2026 as it stands on `origin/main` (3bf8abf), so later changes count. The reviewer did not write the code and worked read-only: a separate session that started from the code and read no requirement, decision, assurance record or pull request description until the findings were drafted. Each finding marked Confirmed was reproduced in a throwaway test, run as the application's database role against a PostgreSQL 16 test database of its own, with the sample of `design-system/SampleData.md` and today set to 18 November 2026. The tests were kept outside the repository and nothing in it was changed. After drafting, `requirements/requirements.md` and `design-system/Decisions.md` were read to say what each finding is: a defect against a stated requirement, a defect against the regulation, or a gap the record already accepts.

The areas read were defects and deferrals, repeat defects, contracts and work orders, release to service, components and life limits, modifications, repairs and service bulletins, weight and balance, configuration set-up, transfers, the Fleet filters, the views of an aircraft's record and its forecast, the programmes list and the sources of directives.

No way was found for one organisation to read or change another's records: a user of the other organisation got Not Found for a defect, a component, a contract and the three views of an aircraft, could not choose our aircraft in its own form, and could not remove our component. Every view that writes checks the role matrix on the server, and the Read Only role was shown no form on any Phase 1 page. Every new table is under row-level security and writes history, with each column that refers to another record checked against its organisation. No signed or issued record was found that the application or its database role could alter afterwards: a certificate of release, a signed baseline, a weighing, a closed defect, an ended contract, a closed work order, an embodied change, a decided bulletin, a removed fitting and a completed annual review each refuse. The same form sent twice is refused by each service read.

## Findings

| Number | Finding | How Found | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A removal with no cycles given records that the component gained none.** `RemoveForm` does not require Airframe Cycles When Removed (`components/forms.py:99`), and `remove_component` then writes the cycles the airframe had at fitting (`components/services.py:305-309`). Reproduction: a component with a limit of 10,000 cycles, fitted to G-ABCD at 90 airframe cycles with 9,500 of its own; the airframe then reads 690 and the component shows 10,100 cycles, 100 Cycles Overdue. Removed through the page with the cycles left empty, the fitting keeps 90 and the component is shown off-wing at 9,500 cycles. The removal cannot be corrected afterwards. `FitForm` likewise takes empty cycles as 0. A defect against REQ-231 (a removal records the airframe hours and cycles) and ML.A.305(e), accumulated time | Confirmed | Open |
| 2 | **The next review of a component kept on condition is worked out from the hours of whichever airframe it is now on.** `component_line` adds the review interval to Airframe Hours at the Last Review and takes away the hours of the aircraft the component is fitted to today (`components/services.py:557-566`). Reproduction: an engine reviewed at 9,800.0 on G-DGHI, every 100 hours, shows 25.7 Hours left at 9,874.3. Removed and fitted to an aircraft at 9,805.0 airframe hours, it shows 95.0 Hours left, Review at 9,900.0, with no mark, though it has run 74.3 hours since its review. Fitted to G-EJKL (11,206.8) it shows 1,306.8 Hours Overdue. REQ-233 and Decisions.md line 116 count from the airframe hours of the last review and do not say what happens when the component moves: a gap the record does not cover, and wrong arithmetic once a component is refitted to another aircraft | Confirmed | Open |
| 3 | **The Maintenance Forecast leaves out a near calendar limit of a component when its other limit cannot be projected.** A component's days are given only when every limit can be placed (`components/services.py:594`), and the forecast uses that one figure (`configuration/forecast.py:116-121`). Reproduction: G-DGHI has no planning utilisation; a hose with 2,000 Hours or 60 Months, new on 1 December 2021, has 13 Days left and is Due Soon. Its forecast line reads Not Forecast, No Planning Utilisation, is sorted last and is not in Next 120 Days. A defect against REQ-290 and REQ-291: the days to a calendar limit are known and the line falls within the next 120 days. Decisions.md line 148 speaks only of a limit in hours | Confirmed | Open |
| 4 | **The Fleet takes no account of a component's limit.** `_next_due` gathers programme tasks, deferred defects and the review certificate only (`aircraft/views.py:116-157`). Reproduction: a part with a Certified Life Limit of 100 hours fitted to G-BXYZ at 95.0 hours is 13.0 Hours Overdue on 18 November 2026; the Fleet shows G-BXYZ as Serviceable, 100-Hour or Annual Check, 41.0 Hours, and the Due in 30 Days filter does not hold it. The Dashboard and the forecast do show it. REQ-280, REQ-281 and Decisions.md line 112 do not name components, so the record accepts this by silence; against ML.A.503 (an installed component does not exceed its life) it is a gap on the page that opens first for an aircraft | Confirmed | Open |
| 5 | **An aircraft being transferred in moves to the Fleet with no checklist started.** `stops_the_fleet` returns nothing where there is no checklist (`configuration/services.py:177-179`), and starting one is a separate button. Reproduction: as Planning and Records, post Records Received 18 of 18 with Save and Move to Fleet for G-HKLM before Start the Checklist is pressed: the aircraft is in the Fleet, with no contract, no signed baseline and no programme asked for, and a checklist can no longer be started. The Transfers page says An Aircraft Moves to the Fleet Once Every Item Is Complete or Not Applicable. A gap the record accepts: REQ-269 and Decisions.md line 139, an aircraft with no checklist moves as before | Confirmed | Open |
| 6 | **A certificate of release issued while a grounding defect was open can never be recorded.** `record_certificate` refuses while a Not Deferrable defect was found on or before the day of issue and not closed by it (`releases/services.py:159-170`). Reproduction: G-ABCD, Cracked Exhaust found 1 November 2026, Not Deferrable; a tyre change completed and released by independent certifying staff on 5 November; the defect closed on 10 November. The certificate dated 5 November is refused before the closure and still refused after it, so the work stays not in the records and its 30 days run out (ML.A.305(a)). The rule is as REQ-263 and Decisions.md line 108 state it, a reading of ML.A.801(g) that is ours; the consequence, that a certificate which exists cannot be entered, is not addressed there | Confirmed | Open |
| 7 | **An action recorded once on a repeat defect stops every later repeat being marked To Assess.** The latest action for a type rating and chapter is shown whatever its date, and To Assess needs none recorded (`defects/services.py:390`, `415-416`). Reproduction: three chapter 33 defects on the Piper PA-28 type in early 2024, action Replaced Loom recorded 6 March 2024. Four more chapter 33 defects on the type between September and November 2026: the board shows 4, Repeat, no mark, with the action of 2024. As REQ-227 words it (a Repeat with no action recorded is To Assess): a gap the record accepts | Confirmed | Open |
| 8 | **A component refitted with fewer hours than it had when removed is accepted without question.** `fit_component` checks only that the hours given are from 0 to 999,999.9 (`components/services.py:224`); the hours at the last removal are not offered or compared. Reproduction: a part with a Certified Life Limit of 500 hours removed at 490.0 hours and fitted again the next day with 0.0 hours and no new or overhauled day: accepted, 497.4 Hours left, no mark. A fitting cannot be corrected afterwards (Decisions.md line 120). REQ-231 is silent: a gap, with ML.A.305(e) and ML.A.503 behind it | Confirmed | Open |
| 9 | **A change recorded late, with a day of embodiment before the last issue of the weight schedule, is taken as in the schedule.** `in_schedule` compares dates only (`airworthiness_rules/mass.py:43-49`). Reproduction: G-ABCD weighed 10 January 2026, schedule Issue 1 dated 1 November 2026; a change of +5.0 Kilograms embodied 15 October 2026 is recorded afterwards: Complete, and the aircraft is Current, though the issue was made without it. As REQ-256 words it: a gap the record accepts | Confirmed | Open |
| 10 | **The Maintenance Forecast of an aircraft gives a server error for a cycle limit too far off to be a date.** `forecast_date` adds the projected days to today with no guard (`airworthiness_rules/forecast.py:26`). Reproduction: G-ABCD with Planning Cycles a Day of 0.1 and a fitted component with a limit of 999,999 cycles, both accepted by their forms: `/aircraft/<id>/forecast/` returns 500, BeyondCalendarError, 9,999,990 days after 18 November 2026. The Components page, the component's page, the Dashboard and the Fleet still load. Unlikely figures, but allowed ones | Confirmed | Open |
| 11 | **Records Received above Records Expected is dropped without a word.** `records_received` redirects to Transfers whether or not the form was taken (`aircraft/views.py:420-433`). Reproduction: post 999 for G-HKLM (18 expected): a redirect, the count stays 14, and the page gives no reason. An empty or non-numeric value is treated the same | Confirmed | Open |
| 12 | **The Airworthiness Directives page shows Record an Airworthiness Directive to every role.** The link is not under `may_record` (`directives/templates/directives/directives.html:6`), though the two source links added in Phase 1 on the same page are. The server refuses a Read Only user who follows it. The line predates Phase 1; the page was changed in it | Confirmed | Open |
| 13 | **A certificate, contract or deferral dated before an approval was recorded, or in a gap between its ending and being recorded as held again, is accepted.** An approval carries no first day, and one recorded as held again loses the day it ended (`organisations/models.py:191-199`, `organisations/approvals.py:126-128`); `organisations_approval_for_activity` in the database reads the same. Not run. The model's own note accepts the first half as erring toward keeping a record (REQ-206); the gap between two periods is not mentioned | Suspected | Open |

## What Was Looked For and Not Found

- **Dates.** The deferral limit, its marks on and after the limit, the 12 months of a repeat defect, the owner's 14 days, the component's calendar life and the off-wing marks were read against `airworthiness_rules/dates.py`: month ends and 29 February go through `add_months`, and today is the United Kingdom date. Nothing wrong was found.
- **Server errors.** Other than finding 10, each constraint and trigger of the Phase 1 tables is checked first by its service, and the forms hold the same bounds; no other odd input read or tried reached the database unrefused.
- **Migrations.** The Phase 1 migrations add tables, replace trigger functions and, in `reviews/0008`, fill new columns for each organisation in turn with that organisation set, as the owner must. They were read, not rehearsed on a populated database whose owner is not a superuser.
- **Exports.** Every Phase 1 type of record is in the export registry, each with how it belongs to an aircraft.
