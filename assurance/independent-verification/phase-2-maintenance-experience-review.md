# Independent review of assessed maintenance experience

27 November 2026. A separate reviewer inspected evidence entry and withdrawal, route inventory, access controls and the Recency view. The duration recompute is recorded separately.

| Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|
| The experience page was absent from the route inventory | Register it as a read page and record both write routes under the existing Administrator action |
| The view could show a duration result beside an expired or future authorisation without identifying its term | Show recorded dates, scope and term next to the duration result |
| Known errors could remain in the totals | Add a separate withdrawal with its reason. Retain the original assessment and exclude it from the calculation |
| The browser test selected the value instead of the rendered option | Exercise the exact label used on the form |
| The absence of attributed work could be mistaken for the absence of assessed evidence | Give attributed work and assessed duration distinct labels |
| Substitute activity always required a particular registration | Allow evidence without a registration for qualifying substitute activity and retain aircraft type and licence category |

The scope references and withdrawal reasons are records of decisions by the organisation. The release and defect-deferral gates are still outstanding.
