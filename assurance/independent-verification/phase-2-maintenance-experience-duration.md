# Independent recompute of Part-66 maintenance experience duration

27 November 2026. A separate session used the UK CAA rule and the example inputs without seeing the code. These figures address duration only. Competence, licence coverage, activity relevance, aircraft similarity and applicable organisational privileges need their own evidence.

The assessment is at the start of 18 November 2026. The app's date-only convention counts 18 November 2024 through 17 November 2026. The rule specifies the preceding two years. It does not specify these exact date-only endpoints.

| Evidence | Independently credited duration |
|---|---|
| 100 distinct qualifying full days | 100 days |
| 99 days and one half-day | 99.5 days |
| 99 days and two half-days on separate dates | 100 days |
| 99 in-window days and one on 18 November 2024 | 100 days |
| 99 in-window days and one on 17 November 2024 | 99 days |
| 98.5 other days and two distinct 0.75-day activities on the same date | 99.5 days under the one-day-per-date convention |
| 80 direct days and 20 permitted substitution days | 100 days |
| 79 direct days and 21 permitted substitution days | 99 days, of which 20 are substitution |
| 1 January to 30 June 2026 with an approved maintenance organisation | Six complete calendar months |
| 1 January to 31 March and 1 July to 30 September 2026 with approved maintenance organisations | Six complete calendar months |

The one-day cap is a project convention. Duplicated or overlapping evidence only substantiates its actual duration. Date-only records for the assessment day are excluded. Split employment blocks count their complete calendar months. Unresolved partial-month evidence needs an organisation assessment.

Category and type coverage are separate from duration. The AMC specifies relevant activity and coverage by category, authorised aircraft, similar aircraft or rating as applicable. Advance CAA agreement can allow a reduced 50-day route in particular independent certifying circumstances without the ordinary substitution credit. Part-145 retains its own organisational requirement under 145.A.35(c) and CAA CAP2377. This calculation covers the ordinary Part-66 routes.
