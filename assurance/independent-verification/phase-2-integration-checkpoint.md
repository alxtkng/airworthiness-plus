# Phase 2 integration inspection

27 November 2026. An independent reader inspected the combined changes from the Phase 1 baseline.

- Recording review staff now takes the authorisation terms in the same save. Existing review staff retain their recorded terms.
- A withdrawn assessed activity can be corrected under its original reference. Its replacement links to the withdrawn entry, and the database checks that the authorisation, reference and organisation match.
- Staff and Recency retain a person’s recorded authorisations when their Staff group changes.
- Recency labels attributed work outside subsequently recorded employment dates. Work history retains the original attribution.
- Known maintenance authorisation dates are now checked in the release recording and defect-deferral services. The next change applies the scoped evidence check and preserves signed legacy records as explicitly unverified historical evidence.
