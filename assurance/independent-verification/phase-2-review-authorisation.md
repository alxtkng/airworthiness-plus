# Independent review of airworthiness review authorisation terms

27 November 2026. A separate reviewer inspected the authorisation form, issue and restoration, history and exports.

| Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Certificate issue and restoration supervision could use terms before formal acceptance | Require the stated acceptance date and any end date to cover the activity |
| A stale form could clear a recorded expiry | Compare the form's revision under lock and require explicit consent to remove an end date |
| Restoration could use a stale copy of the supervisor’s terms | Lock and re-read both staff records in a consistent order |
| A reviewer's current recency could look like a current authorisation | Display the expiry state alongside a separately labelled Recency Status |
| An aircraft-only export could not independently show the terms at issue | Capture the scope and end date when a new certificate is issued. Existing records retain their own evidence |

The scope is an evidence description. Aircraft eligibility and Part-CAMO approval remain outside this slice.
