## Accessibility Statement for Airworthiness+

This statement applies to Airworthiness+ at [Address of the Service].

Airworthiness Limited wants as many people as possible to be able to use it, including at work on the apron, in gloves, in sunlight and on night shifts. You should be able to:

- change colours, contrast, text size and the size of controls on the Display page, or leave them to your device
- zoom in up to 400% without the text spilling off the screen
- move through every page using a keyboard only
- use the service with a screen reader, such as NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver
- sign in with a password manager or a passkey
