# Accessibility

Every page of Airworthiness+, in the application and on the canvas, meets WCAG 2.2 at Level AA. This is the record: the standard, why it applies, the rules every page meets, the display settings, the contrast results, the audit of every page, and what still needs a person.

## The Standard

- **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, published as ISO/IEC 40500:2025. WCAG 3 is a working draft and does not replace WCAG 2, so nothing is built to it.
- Level AAA is applied in three places only: SC 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions (Reduce Motion), SC 1.4.6 Contrast (Enhanced) in the High contrast setting, and SC 2.5.5 Target Size (Enhanced) in the Comfortable density.
- Requirements REQ-383 to REQ-410 in `requirements/requirements.md`, one for each rule below and each display setting.

## Legal Context

For the record only; not legal advice.

- **Equality Act 2010.** The reasonable adjustments duty on service providers is anticipatory: it is owed to disabled people in general, before any one person asks.
- **European Accessibility Act.** Applies from 28 June 2025, mainly to consumer services. The engineer’s personal logbook would be in scope if it were sold to consumers in the European Union.
- **Business customers** will ask for an Accessibility Conformance Report and an accessibility statement. Outlines are in `assurance/accessibility-conformance-report.md` and `assurance/accessibility-statement.md`.

## Maintenance Conditions

Design for the conditions of the work as well as for permanent disability.

| Condition | What Answers It |
|---|---|
| Gloves and greasy hands | Comfortable density: 44 pixel targets (SC 2.5.5) |
| Glare in sunlight on the apron | High contrast: muted text and lines at 7:1 or more |
| A tablet held one-handed | Reflow at 320 pixels (SC 1.4.10); targets of 24 pixels or more everywhere (SC 2.5.8) |
| Night shifts | Dark appearance, with `#0A0A0A`, not black, behind text |
| Fatigue, one of the Dirty Dozen | Error summary that names each field (SC 3.3.1, 3.3.3); nothing asked twice (SC 3.3.7); words in full (Writing.md) |

About 8% of men and 0.5% of women have a colour vision deficiency, mostly red-green. Colour never carries meaning alone (SC 1.4.1): every status dot has its word.

## How It Is Built

- **Tokens.** Every colour with two themes is written `light-dark(light, dark)` in `app.css`, so the browser’s colour scheme, or the Appearance setting, chooses. `control-border` is the edge of every input, select, text area and secondary button. `gray-400` stays as the divider between rows and sections, which is decorative. `focus-ring`, `focus-width` and `focus-offset` make the one focus outline. Each status dot has an edge token that brings it to 3:1.
- **Base template.** A Skip to Main Content link first on every page, signed in or not; `main` with `tabindex="-1"` as its target; a title on every page, its own or its view, then Airworthiness+.
- **Tables.** Every column heading has `scope="col"`. The script names each table by the heading of its section, and puts each table in a box that scrolls on its own; below 800 pixels a one-line table is at least 640 pixels wide, so its columns stay readable and its links 24 pixels apart. The search above a table shows its Search label. The count of rows shown is a polite live region.
- **Forms.** The `error_summary` tag opens a form sent back with errors on There Is a Problem, with a link to each field. Each message has the identifier Django puts in the field’s `aria-describedby`, and the field has `aria-invalid`. A radio group is linked at its first choice.
- **Canvas.** `scripts/gen.py` carries the same rules into every generated page; `accessible()` applies them to pages built another way, and `scripts/accessible_pages.py` to the hand-built pages. Running either twice changes nothing.

## Display Settings

The device decides first. A person can then choose on the Display page, beside Change Password and Passkeys, and the choice is kept on their account (REQ-410). `base.html` writes each choice on `<html>` as a data attribute; nothing is written for a setting left to the device.

| Setting | Options | Behaviour | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appearance | System, Light, Dark | System follows `prefers-color-scheme`. Dark keeps `#0A0A0A` behind text; the `#000000` sidebar carries `gray-900` at 8.13:1 and `gray-1000` at 17.94:1. The canvas keeps its `dark` property as the preview switch | REQ-405 |
| Contrast | System, Standard, High | System follows `prefers-contrast: more`. High raises muted text and every line to 7:1 or more. Under `forced-colors: active` the active sidebar item, tab, open row and status dot are shown by lines and text | REQ-406 |
| Text Size | Default, Large, Larger | 1, 1.125 and 1.25 times every font size and line height, from the root, independent of browser zoom | REQ-407 |
| Density | Compact, Comfortable | Compact is today’s one-line tables with targets of 24 pixels or more. Comfortable gives 44 pixel targets, 44 pixel controls and 16 pixel cell padding | REQ-408 |
| Reduce Motion | System, On, Off | System follows `prefers-reduced-motion`. The one transition, the turn of a disclosure chevron, becomes 0 milliseconds | REQ-409 |

No dyslexia font is offered. The evidence does not support one, and Geist with good line spacing and left-aligned text already follows the British Dyslexia Association Style Guide.

## Open Decision

- **SC 2.2.1 Timing Adjustable (Level A).** A signed-in session ends after 30 minutes without use, 60 on managed devices, with no warning and no way to extend it. That does not meet SC 2.2.1, and no exception in the criterion covers it. The usual answer, as on GOV.UK, is a warning at least 20 seconds before, with a button to stay signed in. The time limit comes from OWASP ASVS 5.0, 7.3, so this is a decision for Alex King: keep the limit and add the warning, or record the failure. Nothing is changed until then.

## Manual Pass

Automated checks find only part of what WCAG asks. None of the following has been done yet. Each is for a person in a real browser: NVDA with Firefox or Chrome on Windows, and VoiceOver with Safari on a Mac and on an iPad.

1. **Keyboard only, every page (SC 2.1.1, 2.1.2, 2.4.3, 2.4.7).** Tab through the sidebar, tabs, filters, table rows, detail rows and every form, including forms inside an opened row. Check the order follows the page, nothing traps focus, the ring is always visible, and focus returns sensibly after a row closes or a form is sent.
2. **Screen reader, every page type (SC 1.3.1, 2.4.6, 4.1.2, 4.1.3).** Landmarks and headings lists make sense; each table is announced with its name and headers; Show Details announces expanded and collapsed; the row count is read after a search; status words are read with their row.
3. **Every form sent back with an error (SC 3.3.1, 3.3.3).** The summary takes focus and is read; each link moves to its field; the field reads its message. Hand-written forms marked Manual in the audit show refusals in a callout and need this check most.
4. **Text spacing (SC 1.4.12).** Apply the W3C text spacing bookmarklet to every page type; nothing clips or overlaps. One-line cells truncate by design; their full value must be in the detail row.
5. **200% and 400% zoom (SC 1.4.4, 1.4.10)** in Chrome, Firefox and Safari, and Text Size Larger at 200%.
6. **Windows contrast themes (forced colours)** with Aquatic and Desert: the active sidebar item, the active tab, an open row, a chosen filter and focus are all visible.
7. **Redundant entry (SC 3.3.7).** Walk each multi-step process (add an aircraft, set up a programme, Goods In to Stores, an airworthiness review) and check nothing given earlier is asked for again.
8. **Sign-in (SC 3.3.8).** Sign in with 1Password, Bitwarden and the browser’s own password manager; paste a password and a code; sign in with a passkey on Windows Hello and an iPhone.
9. **Gloves and glare.** Use the Comfortable density with work gloves on an iPad, and High contrast outdoors in sunlight.
10. **Canvas.** Open every page in the Design editor in light and dark and check focus, the skip link and the Display page’s two densities.
