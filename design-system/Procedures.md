# Procedures by Approval

What each approval's exposition must contain, from the Civil Aviation Authority's exposition guides, and where Airworthiness+ does each procedure. B.8 Maintenance Data is now its own module. The Exposition page shows the Part-CAO list in full against our modules. Personnel should be familiar with the parts of the exposition relevant to their tasks (AMC2 CAO.A.025(a)), so Required Reading is assigned by part, not always to all staff.

| Approval | Exposition | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Part-CAO | Combined Airworthiness Exposition | AMC1 CAO.A.025; Civil Aviation Authority example CAE (Parts F and G carry the BCAR A8-24 and A8-25 supplements) |
| Part-CAMO | Continuing Airworthiness Management Exposition | CAP 2153, third edition March 2026; checklist SRG1773 |
| Part 145 | Maintenance Organisation Exposition | CAP 2375, Issue 04 March 2026 |
| Part 147 | Maintenance Training Organisation Exposition | CAP 2376, Issue 01 July 2022 |
| Part 21 Subpart G | Production Organisation Exposition | CAP 2967, Edition 2 June 2024 |

Part 21 Subpart J (design) has no exposition guide here yet.

## Part-CAO, 59 Procedures

- Part A, General Description: A.1 to A.13 (statement, presentation, facilities, scope, amendments and changes, alternative means of compliance, management personnel, organisation chart, manpower, lists of certifying, programme, review and permit to fly staff)
- Part B, General Procedures: B.1 to B.12 (quality system, audit plan, maintenance contracts, staff qualification, one-off and limited authorisations, subcontracting, maintenance data, records, airworthiness review, flight conditions, permit to fly)
- Part C, Maintenance Procedures: C.1 to C.15 (general, work order acceptance, components and tools, facility, standards, error prevention, critical tasks, fabrication, release, defects, away from base, component under aircraft rating, installed component, special procedures, review certificate under maintenance privilege)
- Part D, Continuing Airworthiness Management: D.1 to D.13 (general, minimum equipment list, programme, directives, modifications and repairs, pre-flight, defects, contracts and work orders, coordination, mass and balance, review certificate or recommendation, extension, maintenance check flights)
- Part E, Supporting Documents: E.1 to E.6
- The template has no occurrence reporting chapter; the Civil Aviation Authority still expects the procedure.

## Part-CAMO

- Part 0, General Organisation: 0.1 to 0.7 (safety policy and statement, scope, management personnel, chart, changes with and without prior approval, alternative means of compliance)
- Part 1, Continuing Airworthiness Management: 1.1a records system and technical log, 1.1b minimum equipment list, 1.2 programme, 1.3 records, 1.4 directives, 1.5 programme effectiveness, 1.6 non-mandatory modifications and inspections, 1.7 repairs and modifications, 1.8 defect reports, 1.9 engineering activity, 1.10 reliability programmes, 1.11 pre-flight inspections, 1.12 aircraft weighing, 1.13 maintenance check flights
- Part 2, Management System: 2.1 hazard identification and risk management, 2.2 internal safety reporting, 2.3 safety action planning, 2.4 safety performance monitoring, 2.5 change management, 2.6 safety training and promotion, 2.7 immediate safety action and emergency response, 2.8 compliance monitoring, 2.9 personnel competency, 2.10 record-keeping, 2.11 occurrence reporting
- Part 3, Contracted Maintenance: 3.1 contractor selection, 3.2 product audit of aircraft
- Part 4, Airworthiness Review: 4.1 to 4.8 (staff, records review, physical survey, imports, recommendations, issue, records, extension); Part 4B Permit to Fly: 4B.1 to 4B.3
- Part 5, Supporting Documents: 5.1 to 5.9 (samples and technical log, review staff, subcontractors with Means of Active Control tables, contracted organisations, contracts, approved programmes, alternative means, SRG1773 checklist, complexity matrix)

## Part 145

- Part 0: contents, effective pages, amendment record, distribution list
- Part 1, Management: 1.1 statement (access, immediate reaction to a safety problem), 1.2 safety policy, 1.3 and 1.4 management personnel and duties (including Safety Manager and Non-Destructive Testing Level 3), 1.5 chart, 1.6 certifying, support and review staff lists, 1.7 manpower, 1.8 facilities, 1.9 scope of work, 1.10 and 1.11 changes with and without prior approval, 1.12 alternative means of compliance
- Part 2, Maintenance Procedures: 2.1 suppliers and subcontractors, 2.2 acceptance and inspection of components, 2.3 storage, tagging and release to maintenance, 2.4 to 2.6 tools (acceptance, calibration, use), 2.7 working environment, 2.8 maintenance data (2.8.2 the organisation's own instructions and notices, with a procedure to ensure staff awareness), 2.9 to 2.12 repairs, scheduled maintenance, directives, modifications, 2.13 maintenance documentation, 2.14 technical records, 2.15 defects, 2.16 release to service, 2.17 records for the customer, 2.18 occurrence reporting, 2.19 and 2.20 defective components, 2.21 computer records, 2.22 manhour planning, 2.23 critical tasks, 2.24 specific procedures, 2.25 maintenance errors, 2.26 shift and task handover, 2.27 data inaccuracies, 2.28 production planning, 2.29 airworthiness review, 2.30 fabrication, 2.31 component under aircraft rating, 2.32 away from base, 2.33 line or base assessment
- Part 3, Management System: 3.1 to 3.7 safety management (3.6.2 every new starter reads the exposition and signs for it), 3.8 compliance monitoring, 3.9 to 3.14 staff qualification and records, 3.15 and 3.16 deviations and concessions, 3.17 specialised activities, 3.18 external working teams, 3.19 competence assessment, 3.20 on the job training, 3.21 licence recommendations, 3.22 record keeping
- Part 4: 4.1 commercial operators, 4.2 customer interface
- Part 5: 5.1 sample documents, 5.2 subcontractors, 5.3 line stations, 5.4 contracted organisations, 5.5 alternative means used

## Part 147

- Part 1, Management: 1.1 to 1.11 (commitment, personnel and duties including Training, Quality and Examination Managers, instructors, assessors, invigilators, chart, staff list, locations, subcontractors, facilities, courses, changes, amendments)
- Part 2, Training and Examination: 2.1 to 2.20 (courses, material, classrooms, workshops, conduct of training, records, examinations and security, practical assessments, marking, results, certificates, subcontractors, occurrence reporting)
- Part 3, Quality: 3.1 to 3.9 (audits of training and examinations, analysis, remedial action, Accountable Manager annual review, qualifying instructors, examiners and assessors, their records, audit personnel)
- Part 4: forms, course syllabi, cross reference index

## Part 21 Subpart G

- Part 1, Management: 1.1 to 1.13 (statement, safety policy, personnel and duties, chart, certifying staff, manpower, facilities, scope and key processes, significant changes, amendments, management system overview, outside parties, permit to fly)
- Part 2, Production: 2.1 airworthiness coordination with the design organisation, 2.2 incoming inspection, 2.3 stores, 2.4 identification and traceability, 2.5 tools, jigs and test equipment, 2.6 production processes, 2.7 inspection and test, 2.8 non-conforming items, 2.9 record completion, 2.10 handling and packing, 2.11 off-site work, 2.12 technical records, 2.13 critical parts, 2.14 release, 2.15 occurrence reporting
- Part 3, Management System: 3.1 documents (3.1.1 distribution, 3.1.2 issue, approval and amendment), 3.2 safety and quality assurance, 3.3 suppliers, 3.4 staff competency and authorisation

## Not Yet in Airworthiness+

From the Part-CAO list: flight conditions and permit to fly (B.11, B.12), fabrication (C.8), minimum equipment list (D.2), maintenance check flights (D.13), technical log (E.4). The Part-CAMO and Part 145 lists add engineering activity, reliability programmes, shift and task handover, data inaccuracy reports, external working teams, concessions and customer interface. Part 147 and Part 21 Subpart G need their own modules (courses and examinations; production and conformity).
