# Writing

The content style guide for every word in Airworthiness+.

## Case and Length

Every line in the interface is a terse label in Title Case. No sentences, no full stops.

- **Labels, not sentences.** "Deferred by the pilot-in-command on 3 November." becomes "Deferred", with "Pilot-in-Command, 3 November" on the line under it.
- **Title Case everywhere:** headings, buttons, tabs, table headers, cell values, notes and statuses. These stay lower case except as the first or last word: a, an, and, as, at, but, by, for, if, in, nor, of, on, or, per, the, to, via, with.
- **Hyphenated words** take a capital on each part except short joining words: Pilot-in-Command, Pilot-Owner, One-Off, Light-Emitting Diode.
- **The registration column is "Aircraft Registration",** never "Aircraft" or "Registration".
- **Units and abbreviations are spelled out:** Kilograms, Maximum Take-Off Mass, Type Certificate Holder, Very High Frequency Radio. Regulatory identifiers such as Part-CAO, CAA Form 1 and Mode S stay as they are.
- **Identifiers keep their own form:** G-ABCD, WP-2026-047, PA-28-140, B1.
- **Aircraft types follow SRG1782 exactly,** the CAA UK Part-66 Type Rating List, and are never re-cased or shortened. The type rating endorsement is the type, such as "Piper PA-28 Series (Lycoming)" or "Robin DR 400 series (Lycoming)". The model is not shown once the type rating is, except when choosing a type in Add an Aircraft. TC holders keep their listed form, such as PIPER AIRCRAFT.
- **Detail goes on the second line,** in Label 13 or Label 14 `gray-900`, never in a longer first line.

## Typography

- Curly apostrophes and quotation marks: Manufacturer’s, not Manufacturer's.
- En dash for ranges with no spaces: 20–100 Newton Metres, 9–10 November.
- True minus sign for negative values: −0.3 Kilograms.
- Commas, not full stops, to join two facts in one label: "Placarded, Daylight Only".
- A colon only to introduce a definition: "Repeat: 3 of the Same Defect on One Type in 12 Months".

## Never Abbreviate

Write everything in full: airworthiness review certificate, not ARC; certificate of release to service, not CRS; Compliance Monitoring Manager, not CMM. The exceptions are licence categories written as the regulation writes them (B1, B2, A2), registrations (G-ABCD), Part names (Part-ML, Part-CAO, Part 145) and document names (CAP 2375, CAA Form 1).

## Dates, Hours and Numbers

- Dates: 18 November 2026. With a weekday: Thursday 19 November. Never 18/11/26.
- Hours: 6,412.6 hours, with a comma for thousands and one decimal place. Remaining time: 12.4 hours, 15 days, 3 months.
- Mass: +1.2 kilograms. Money: £1,250.
- References in Geist Mono: WP-2026-047, 2026-F04, 2026-0007.

## People and Roles

- A person with their qualification: Sam Patel, B1. A role on its own is capitalised: Continuing Airworthiness Manager, Safety Manager.
- Owners are pilot-owner, syndicate or flying club.

## Placeholders and Example Data

- Anything not real goes in square brackets: [Reference], [Owner Name], [Your Organisation].

## Punctuation

- No middle dots, no slashes as separators in text (breadcrumbs excepted), no exclamation marks.
- British English: organisation, licence, programme, colour.

## No Taglines

Never write marketing copy inside the product or the design system: no slogans, no pairs of abstract adjectives ("clear and correct", "simple and powerful"), no alliteration. If a line is needed, state a fact: what it is, who it is for, or what regulation it covers. If there is no fact to state, leave the line out.
