# CAP 562 Notes, Chunk 07 (pages 677 to 794)

Chunk covers Book 2: Chapter 8 (Weighing), Chapter 12 (Servicing, Routine Maintenance) and the first part of Chapter 20 (Standard Practices, Airframe). Almost all leaflets dated 15 April 2011 (20-40 page 1-2 dated 30 November 2011). Mostly maintenance-practice content. The only substantial BCAR/regulatory content is in Leaflet 8-10 (weighing requirements, Weight and Balance Report, Weight and Centre-of-Gravity Schedule).

## 2. BCAR and National Regime

Only Leaflet 8-10 contains regime content:

- Weighing requirements and the Weight and Balance Schedule are prescribed in **BCAR Section A, Chapters A5-4, A6-4 and A7-10**; the Load and Distribution Schedule alternative must comply with **BCAR Section A, Chapter A7-10 and Section B, Chapter B7-10** (Section B being the design/construction side referenced for light aircraft load data).
- **BCAR A6-4** is cited for the fleet mean weight alternative to individual periodic check weighing.
- **BCAR Chapter A6-2** is cited (Leaflet 12-10 para 6.1 note) for **duplicate inspections** where any part of aircraft or engine control system is inside a closed-in compartment.
- **Certificate of Airworthiness (C of A)**: the CAA cannot issue a C of A for a prototype, variant (prototype modified) or series aircraft above 5700 kg MTWA until a **Weight and Balance Report** is prepared by a **"CAA Approved Organisation"** (no specific A8-x approval type named).
- c.g. limits are in the **Flight Manual** or other document associated with the C of A such as the **Owner's Manual**; if no such document, limits are specified in the **C of A** itself (relevant for older national aircraft lacking a flight manual).
- MTWA definition ties to "the Certificate of Airworthiness or Flight Manual".
- **Air Navigation Order (ANO)** and **Air Navigation (General) Regulations**: Commander must be satisfied load is safe; assumed vs actual weights rule (see section 3).
- Schedule copy goes to the **"CAA Safety Regulation Group"** (historic CAA organisational name).
- Signatory: "authorised representative of a CAA Approved Organisation or a person suitably qualified and acceptable to the CAA"; example schedule signature line "Inspector/Engineer on behalf of ... Approval reference ...".
- Leaflet 20-40 notes it addresses UK-designed aircraft with read-across to other states of design and supports information published by "the type certificate holder or equivalent".
- Leaflet 20-30: older aircraft may have SBAC AS-spec cable components; "approval may be granted" to use equivalent BS parts, but the complete cable control run may have to be changed (relevant to legacy/historic national aircraft).

No content in this chunk on Permits to Fly, LAA/BMAA, ex-military, State aircraft, or A8-x approval types.

## 3. Obligations and Numbers

### Weighing (8-10)
- Weigh every aircraft to determine Basic Weight and c.g. when all manufacturing processes are complete.
- Aircraft **> 5700 kg (12 500 lb) MTWA**: re-weigh **within 2 years after date of manufacture**, then check weighing at intervals **not exceeding 5 years**, and whenever the CAA requires.
- Aircraft **<= 5700 kg MTWA**: re-weigh **as required by the CAA** (CAA considers history, flying performance, effect of major overhaul, modification, repair or replacement).
- Sampling-basis weighing (representative aircraft for others of same standard) allowed by agreement with CAA.
- Fleet mean weight / fleet mean c.g. alternative: initial mean of all aircraft of type in fleet, **revised annually by sample weighing** (A6-4).
- Recorded load at weighing must not differ significantly from Basic Equipment List; CAA may require actual weights of Variable Load items if operating weight differs significantly from Basic Weight not accountable to mods/repairs.
- All weighing records and calculations available to CAA; retained by manufacturer, overhauler or operator; **previous weighing records must not be destroyed** but retained with aircraft records; operators must record all known weight and c.g. changes after weighing.
- Weight and Balance Report required before C of A for prototype/variant/series aircraft **> 5700 kg MTWA**, prepared by CAA Approved Organisation.
- Weight and Centre-of-Gravity Schedule required for each aircraft **> 2730 kg (6000 lb) MTWA**; for **<= 2730 kg** either the Schedule or a Load and Distribution Schedule (A7-10 / B7-10). New aircraft **2730 to 5700 kg**: Parts B and C may be given in the Weight and Balance Report.
- Operator must issue revised Schedule when weight/c.g. change exceeds the maximum figure agreed by CAA for the type; if not re-weighed, state it is calculated from last Weight and Balance Report plus known changes; keep calculation record.
- Copy of Schedule retained by operator plus copy sent to CAA (with Basic Equipment list). For aircraft **<= 5700 kg**, a copy must be in the Flight Manual; if no Flight Manual is required, display or keep in an identified stowage in the aircraft.
- Basic Weight may include usable oil for turbine-engined aircraft and aircraft of MTWA not exceeding 5700 kg.
- Weighing apparatus checked, adjusted and certified by a competent authority at periods **not exceeding 1 year**; zero check before every weighing.
- Electrical weighing cells often switched on **30 minutes** before weighing; second weighing essential with hydrostatic units/load cells; ramps approx **6%**; very large aircraft may need **18 or more** platforms.
- Loading: if aircraft **> 5700 kg MTWA or seating 12 or more persons**, loading may use assumed weights for persons and baggage; otherwise actual weights (example schedule note: actual pilot weight required for aircraft not exceeding 5700 kg or with less than 12 seats).
- Densities in example: petrol 7.2 lb/imp gal; kerosene 8.1 lb/imp gal; oil 9.0 lb/imp gal.
- Sign conventions: horizontal minus forward / plus aft of datum; vertical minus below / plus above; transverse minus right / plus left.
- Formulas: c.g. = total moment / total weight; c.g. from main wheels = A x W1 / W2; %SMC = (A - B)/C x 100; schedule moments often expressed in lb in/100.

### Cleanliness (12-10)
- Engineer witnesses closing of a structure and endorses the inspection stage on the history card; endorses aircraft cleanliness on aircraft history sheet. Recommended final loose-article inspection immediately after first flight following manufacture or overhaul.
- Floor panels removed for underfloor inspection at Maintenance Schedule intervals.
- After livestock carriage, possible Health Authority contact for fumigation; several hours ventilation after disinfestation.
- Fuel work: check low pressure filters and booster pump screens after filling tanks and again after initial engine runs.
- Chlorinated solvents (trichlorethylene, carbon tetrachloride) not to be used inside aircraft.
- Discard containers of compound that have been open for an unknown time.

### Torque (20-10)
- Initial assembly high torque: tighten to about half, slacken, retighten to full.
- Sealant joints: torque within sealant application time; recheck after **10 minutes** but within **twice the application life**.
- Union nuts (flared): torque, slacken half a turn, retorque. Flareless: finger tight then **one sixth to one third of a turn**.
- Split pins/lock wire: use low side of range; if max reached without slot alignment, change nut and washer. Never slacken to align.
- Extension formula: scale reading = specified torque x L/(L+X); if diverging more than **15°**, use direct distance D. 1 lb in = 1.15 kg cm = 0.113 Nm.
- Over-torqued fasteners: remove, render unserviceable and scrap (both bolt and nut); never back off and retorque.
- Torque wrenches checked for accuracy before use or at intervals **normally not exceeding 6 months** (more often if heavily used); beam wrenches zero-checked before use. Avoid first quarter of scale.

### Cable (20-30)
- Reel barrel diameter at least **40 x cable diameter**; coils at least **50 x cable diameter**, minimum **150 mm (6 in)**.
- Swaging squeeze approx **0.18 mm (0.007 in)**; swage to within approx **1.2 mm (0.050 in)** of inspection hole; rotate approx **50°**; withdraw **1.6 mm (0.0625 in)** per cycle.
- Over-swaged (too small diameter): reject cable and fitting.
- Crack inspection with **10x lens** or magnetic/dye penetrant. First fitting in a production batch usually sectioned after proof loading.
- Talurit splice approx **90%** of breaking strength (BS W9/W11); only replaces same type splice or hand splices, never where swaged end fittings were used.
- Manual splice must not fail below **80%** of breaking strength; allow approx **23 cm (9 in)** per splice up to 3.2 mm cable, **30 cm (12 in)** for 4.0 to 6.4 mm.
- Proof load: British practice **50%**, American practice **60%** of declared minimum breaking strength; hold **30 seconds** (swaged), up to **3 minutes** (splices).
- Length measured after proof loading; cables longer than **120 cm (4 ft)** tensioned with approx **550 N (112 lbf) or 2% of breaking load, whichever is least**.
- Competency: trial swagings / initial competency test, periodic representative samples tensile tested to destruction.
- Tables of breaking loads (e.g. 1/8 in 7x19 carbon 2000 lbf, CR 1760 lbf, BS W13 7.85 kN; 1/4 in 7000/6400 lbf, 28.40 kN).

### Chains (20-40)
- Proof load one third of minimum breaking load: 8 mm (BS No.1) 800 lb / 267; 0.375 in (No.2) 1900 / 634; 0.50 in (No.4) 1800 / 600; 0.50 in (No.6) 3500 / 1166.
- Elongation check tensile loads: 12, 16, 28, 28 lb respectively. Extension = (M - X x P)/(X x P) x 100; **reject/replace whole run if > 2%** on any section.
- Chain same-end termination: odd number of pitches; endless chain: even number. No cranked links, no spring clip connecting links; split pins and peened nuts/bolts not re-used.
- O-ring chain: brown dust (rust wear products) means unserviceable, change immediately.
- Proof loading not needed after routine removal; needed if partial replacement.
- Lubricant DTD 417B (obsolescent).

### Hose (20-50)
- Rubber/synthetic hose storage life **3 to 5 years**; PTFE normally no storage life; flexible metallic in sealed envelopes no life limitation. Bulk hose life from **cure date**; assemblies from **date of manufacture or assembly**.
- Storage temperature **10°C to 26°C**; FIFO stock rotation mandatory.
- Straight hose assemblies at least **3% longer** than maximum distance between fittings; never in tension. Flexing radius generally **twice** the static bend radius.
- Life types: hard life (scrap regardless), overhaul life, on condition, as per approved Maintenance Schedule; at each inspection check that prescribed life remains valid until next inspection.
- Pressure test **1.5 x maximum working pressure**; non-flexing hose flexed about **15°** each way, pressure held at least **2 minutes**; flexing hose through normal angle plus 15°. Blister test (hydraulic): 1.5 x max working pressure for 2 minutes.
- Ball test ball approx **90%** of end fitting bore.
- Bonding: not exceeding **0.050 ohm or 0.025 ohm per foot, whichever greater** (re-usable fittings); flexible metallic installed **0.050 ohm**.
- Metallic hose test: no pressure drop after **5 minutes**; pneumatic immersed in water approx **26°C** for 5 minutes; length check after at least **1 hour** unconfined; drying in forced-draught oven **30 minutes at 100°C to 105°C**.
- Fire-zone hoses need fire-resistant sleeves. Operating ambient around **-55°C** at altitude.

### Rigid pipes (20-60)
- Clearances: **6 mm (0.25 in)** from fixed structure, **18 mm (0.75 in)** from control rods and rigid moving parts, **25 mm (1.0 in)** from control cables.
- Hose connection gap between pipes **6 to 12 mm (0.25 to 0.5 in)**.
- External swage repair gap up to **8 mm (0.3 in)**; internal swaging mandrel a further **10 turns** after contact.
- Coupling repairs only when specified in Maintenance Manual (resonance/fatigue risk).

### Pneumatic (20-70)
- Pressures up to **24 MN/m2 (3,500 lbf/in2)**; older two-stage approx **3 MN/m2 (450 lbf/in2)**.
- Oil-and-water trap drained **daily**, or **after each flight** in freezing conditions.
- Leak rate check: fully pressurise, recheck after **12 hours** (or specified time), record initial/final pressures with ambient temperature; compare against manufacturer maximum leakage rate.
- Desiccant emergency re-activation **250°C to 300°C for 4 to 5 hours** if permitted.
- Unscrew drain plugs/connections max **a quarter turn** under pressure.
- Storage bottles pressure tested at specified periods; test date stamped on bottle neck. Damage beyond protective treatment renders bottle unserviceable.

## 4. Records, Forms and Documents

- **Weight and Balance Report** (8-10 para 2.8): prepared by CAA Approved Organisation; contents: reference number and date; designation, manufacturer's number, nationality and registration marks; copy of Weighing Record; copy of Weight and Centre-of-Gravity Schedule (with Basic Equipment List if separate from Part A); diagram/description of datum points and relation to fuselage frame numbering and SMC (= MAC); lever arms for Disposable Load items (fuel/oil/fluids per tank including agricultural hoppers, passenger seats per layout, baggage holds); significant c.g. effects of configuration change (e.g. gear retraction). Applies to aircraft as delivered from manufacturer to operator; enables operator's ANO loading instructions.
- **Weighing Record**: equipment and items of load (fluid in tanks) at weighing, readings, calculations; retained permanently with aircraft records.
- **Weight and Centre-of-Gravity Schedule**: Part A Basic Weight (Basic Weight, c.g., total moment, unusable fuel/oil included, Basic Equipment List items with weight and lever arm); Part B Variable Load (items, weight, arm, moment per role, e.g. pilot, life-jackets, seats, stretcher); Part C Loading Information/Disposable Load (fuel tanks with capacity, oil, baggage, passenger rows, moment change on gear retraction, densities). Must show aircraft designation, nationality and registration marks (or MSN), date of issue, signature of authorised representative of CAA Approved Organisation or acceptable person, supersession statement, reference to the Weight and Balance Report on which based. Example header fields: Reference, Produced by, Aircraft Designation, Registration, Manufacturer, Manufacturer's Serial, MTWA, c.g. limits (Flight Manual reference). Footer: date prepared, supersedes previous issues, Signed (Inspector/Engineer), on behalf of, Approval reference.
- **Load and Distribution Schedule**: alternative for aircraft <= 2730 kg (BCAR A7-10 / B7-10).
- **Basic Equipment List**.
- **Load Sheet**: per flight for large aircraft; Basic Weight, Variable Load items, Operating Weight, Disposable Load (passengers by class, cargo by hold), Zero Fuel Weight, fuel by tanks, Take-off Weight, each with weight, arm, moment and %SMC.
- **Flight Manual / Owner's Manual / C of A**: c.g. limits source; Schedule copy in Flight Manual (<= 5700 kg).
- **Component history cards and aircraft build sheets**: must contain clause requiring certification that each part inspected and free from loose articles etc.; engineer endorses closing inspection on history card; aircraft history sheet endorsement for complete aircraft cleanliness.
- **Tool inventory** of tools/spares/equipment taken to aircraft, checked on completion.
- **Cable assembly identification**: tag or sleeve marked with cable part number (Leaflet E-10), evidence of proof loading.
- **Hose markings**: date of manufacture (stencil, tab/band, coloured braid strands), drawing/part number, inspection stamp, test stamp, manufacturer name; lay lines. Metallic hose: tag/brass band/label with part number, date of manufacture, manufacturer, inspector's stamp; after test an additional band/tag with date of test and inspection stamp, and recommended reference in **log book** or other records.
- **Rigid pipes**: inspector's stamp adjacent to part number as evidence of prior inspection/testing.
- **Test equipment check records** (20-50 para 8.2.1): test equipment and instruments checked at regular intervals and record kept.
- **Pneumatic components**: container marked with date of packing, record of tests carried out, storage life; storage bottles stamped with test date.
- Torque wrench and weighing equipment calibration (certified by competent authority, 1 year for weighing; 6 months torque wrenches).

## 5. Practice Guidance

- **Weighing (8-10)**: weigh in closed hangar after temperature stabilisation; in open only on firm level site, minimal wind, dry aircraft, multiple readings. Level in rigging position using jig points; large aircraft level longitudinally and laterally. Deduct levelling equipment weight; tail-wheel negative tail load via spring balance (minus quantity, add balance/rope weight). Equipment types: weighbridge, hydrostatic units, electrical strain gauge cells, electronic platforms (zero before/after). Jack weighing per manufacturer and Leaflet G-10. Rotorcraft: datum usually at main rotor centroid, moments +/-; single-point hydrostatic suspension; rotor head locks weight included. Changes to Basic Equipment: compute new weight and moment (sign rules), weigh mod parts including brackets, sealant etc. if Modification Leaflet lacks data, then revise Schedule Part A. Small aircraft: check c.g. at maximum and minimum fuel; extreme forward/aft loading cases. Large aircraft: curtailed envelope (fuel usage, SG, seating allowance, flight allowance, gear/flap moment), loading order, floor loading and lashing.
- **Cleanliness (12-10)**: FOD hazards (control jams, tank damage, electrical shorts, filter blockage, oxygen explosion risk with oil/grease). Closed-pocket overalls, mats, tool trays, no refreshments on aircraft, keep containers closed, unpack parts only when needed, keep blanks fitted, segregate stripping from assembly. Exterior washing (non-atomised spray, no undiluted solvent on acrylics, relubricate after washing), heavy exhaust deposits, de-icing salts washed off promptly, salt-air programmes (more frequent washing, compressor wash), radioactive contamination monitoring with Geiger counter for very high flying aircraft. Interior: pressurised cabins condensation, polyester foam acid corrosion (lift stained foam, inspect, renew), livestock disinfection. Parked: covers/blanks with streamers; turbine intake/jet pipe covers always fitted when not in use; engine rotation or inhibition depending on time out of service. Closed structures: progressive inspection (Leaflet 51-50), vacuum not air jets, shake small surfaces, radiography for boxed structures, duplicate inspection where controls involved (A6-2), check drain holes. Systems: blanks designed so system cannot be connected with blank in place; no textile, paper, wood, cork; polythene bags short term only; wipe surplus grease off control cables at fairleads/pulleys. Ground rigs filtration at least equal to aircraft system; check fuel dispensers and bulk storage for water.
- **Torque (20-10)**: follow manufacturer lubricated vs dry values; hold bolt, turn nut (if torquing bolt side, use high side of range); stiffnut friction check by hand or measure in-built torque; add stiffnut frictional torque where old manuals give only non-locking values; ratchet floating-drive direction; retorque by backing off part of a turn.
- **Thread inserts (20-20)**: only where specified; special taps and GO/NO GO gauges from insert manufacturer; outer coil at least half a pitch below surface; NDT for cracks near edges; SBAC(AS) inserts with BA/BSF/BSP threads have yellow-painted tang; five standard lengths (1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3 x diameter); identification by measurement impractical. Thin-wall: key-locked (replacement keys in different positions), swaged, ring-locked (insert 0.25 to 0.5 mm below surface, ring flush).
- **Cable (20-30)**: preformed cable specs; cut mechanically, never by flame; swaging procedure and 8-point inspection (combination, diameter by GO/NOT GO gauge, engagement via inspection hole/paint mark, lay undisturbed, shank smooth, proof load, crack check, length/identification/protection); lanolin dip; Talurit ferrule material (aluminium alloy with carbon steel, copper with CR steel), flash check; manual splicing tuck sequence and inspection (no light between strands, bend resistance, thimble tight); proof load rig with trough, paint at fitting entry to detect slip; crack check by Leaflet F-50 (magnetic) or F-20 (penetrant for stainless).
- **Chains (20-40)**: obtain as complete proof-loaded units from approved manufacturers; never break riveted links; disconnect only at bolted/screwed design breakdown points; non-reversible system five features (chain, wheel shroud, wheel positioning, guard, non-interchangeable connectors); post-install checks (twist, alignment, pre-tension just enough to remove backlash, guarding, split pins, lubrication, wheel security); maintenance checks (smoothness, wear, damage, corrosion, no twisting on adjustment); removal inspection (clean in paraffin and hot-air dry, elongation, kinks, articulation through 180°, deterioration); storage soaked in oil, coiled, greaseproof paper.
- **Hose (20-50)**: use only specified hose; fire-resistant sleeves in fire zones; store flat, away from light/motors; preformed/PTFE keep shape (lock wire between ends); correct blanks (no tape/rag); pre-install checks (damage, corrosion, part number, date of manufacture and last test, bore by light or ball test, flushing); installation (clearance, minimum bend radius, no sharp bend at fitting, two-spanner tightening, lay line for twist, support guides, minimal taping, never leather); brake hoses checked at full strut extension; post-install flow, pressure, bonding tests. Inspection rejection criteria: cover cracks penetrating to braid, exposed/damaged braid, kinks (scrap), corroded braid or fittings (scrap), contamination (swelling, sponginess), overheating patches, leaks (scrap), blisters subject to test. Re-usable end fittings procedure (socket left-hand thread, mark at socket entry, nipple with pilot or mandrel extending 6 mm, lip-seal variant). Flexible metallic: no internal brushing, non-chlorinated solvent clean, no tightening mechanical fittings to stop leak, inspection list (braid distortion, leaks, abrasion, twist, corrosion, cracks, hot-gas discoloration).
- **Rigid pipes (20-60)**: identification per BS M23; avoid U bends; check type, inspector's stamp, flare/preset, corrosion; blend light external corrosion if permitted, reject internal/deep; oxygen pipes degreased; clamps and packing (no leather); bonding strips; never force or draw pipes with union nuts; two spanners; pressure and flow tests after installation; soap solution leak test then wash off; exposed pipes (wheel bays) inspected carefully; chafing under clamps; oval section at bends leading to cracks; rubber hose couplings re-tighten clips after short service.
- **Pneumatic (20-70)**: release pressure before disconnecting (pause after quarter turn); ground locks before releasing down lines, label gear and emergency selectors; electrical isolation and placarding; after compressor failure check downstream filters for metal contamination; check part number, mod state and storage life before installation; new gaskets; observe flow direction on NRVs; full functioning test (rigging, micro-switches, actuator not bottoming, grease filled actuators); component testing only with proper rigs (aircraft system not acceptable alternative); system tests after any adjustment/dismantle/renewal and at scheduled periods; compressor output check by ground engine run against MM tables; external charging supply must have oil-and-water traps and preferably a dehydrator; filter servicing; electronic leak detectors (ultrasonic or ion with carbon tetrachloride tracer).

## 6. Software Implications

- **WeighingEvent** object: aircraft, date, location (hangar/open), method (weighbridge, hydrostatic, electrical cells, electronic platforms, jacks, single-point rotorcraft), equipment IDs with calibration due dates (max 1 year), reactions per point, arms, deductions (levelling equipment, tare), fluids state, configuration, computed Basic Weight, c.g., total moment (lb in or lb in/100), %SMC, performed by organisation/approval reference. Previous records must be immutable/never deleted.
- **Re-weigh due alert**: if MTWA > 5700 kg, first due 2 years from date of manufacture, then every 5 years from last weighing; flag "as required by CAA" for <= 5700 kg; support CAA-directed ad hoc requirement; support fleet mean weight scheme with annual sample weighing due date and sampling-basis flag.
- **WeightAndCGSchedule** object: issue number, date, supersedes previous, based-on Weight and Balance Report reference/date, Parts A/B/C line items (item, weight, lever arm, moment, capacity), Basic Equipment List, signatory (name, organisation, approval reference), status (current/superseded), "calculated not weighed" flag, distribution (operator copy, CAA copy, Flight Manual copy or aircraft stowage for <= 5700 kg). Schedule type rule: required > 2730 kg; <= 2730 kg allow Load and Distribution Schedule.
- **Weight and balance change log**: each mod/repair/equipment add/remove/relocate with weight, arm, moment delta, cumulative change vs CAA-agreed threshold for the type; alert when cumulative change exceeds threshold requiring revised schedule (or re-weigh).
- **W&B calculator**: datum, sign conventions, unit consistency enforcement (lb/in or kg/m), SMC/MAC parameters, envelope check at max and min fuel, assumed vs actual masses rule (> 5700 kg or >= 12 seats), densities reference list (petrol 7.2, kerosene 8.1, oil 9.0 lb/imp gal).
- **Tooling/calibration register**: torque wrenches (accuracy check max 6 months, more frequent for heavy use; zero check for beam type), weighing equipment (1 year), pressure test rigs and gauges (regular, recorded), bonding testers, swaging machines, GO/NO GO gauges.
- **Tool control / FOD**: tool inventory checkout and check-in per job; close-up inspection task with engineer sign-off ("closing witnessed") on task card; duplicate inspection flag for control systems in closed compartments (A6-2); post-first-flight loose-article inspection task after manufacture/overhaul.
- **Hose assembly component record**: part number, cure date / date of manufacture or assembly, last test date, life type (hard life / overhaul life / on-condition), life limit, storage life (3 to 5 years rubber; none PTFE/sealed metallic), installed location, next inspection; alert if life will expire before next scheduled inspection. Stores FIFO.
- **Shelf-life tracking in stores**: hose, pneumatic components (storage life driven by seals, marked on container), sealants/compounds (discard open containers of unknown age), desiccant tins; storage condition fields (10 to 26°C for hose).
- **Cable/chain assemblies**: part number, proof load test record (load, % of MBS, hold time), length after proof load, identification tag; chain elongation check result (% vs 2% limit) with reject status; non-reversible chain flag.
- **Test record templates**: hose pressure test (test medium, pressure = 1.5 x MWP, duration 2 or 5 min, flex angle, result), ball/flow test, bonding resistance (limits 0.050 ohm / 0.025 ohm per ft), drying, test tag applied, log book entry. Pneumatic leak rate test (initial/final pressure, ambient temps, 12 h, max permissible rate). Compressor output test.
- **Recurring task templates**: oil-and-water trap drain daily / after each flight in freezing conditions; storage bottle drain and pressure test periodic; dehydrator desiccant change (hours, experience-based); filter clean/renew; underfloor inspection; chain inspection and removal inspection; hose in-situ inspection; washing frequency increase for salt-air operations; Geiger monitoring for very high altitude aircraft; engine rotation/inhibition for parked aircraft.
- **Defect/reject reasons reference list** for hoses (kink, corroded braid, contamination, overheating, leak, cracks to braid), cables (over-swaged, slip, crack), chains (>2% extension, kink/twist, tight joint, brown dust on O-ring chain), fasteners (over-torqued: scrap both bolt and nut).
- **Reference lists**: specs BS W9, W11, W12, W13, MIL-W-83420 (formerly MIL-W-1511, MIL-L-5424), BS 228 / ISO 606 (withdrawn), SBAC AS, AGS, DTD 417B (obsolescent), BS M23 pipe identification; insert types; cable breaking load tables; chain proof load tables.
- Status for documents: "obsolescent" (retained for servicing existing equipment).
