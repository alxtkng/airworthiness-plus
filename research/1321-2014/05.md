## 1. Scope

The chunk covers when and how an aircraft or component is released to service outside Part-145 (Subpart H). It also covers the periodic airworthiness review and the Airworthiness Review Certificate (ARC), including validity, extension, import, transfer and findings (Subpart I).

### Subpart H — Certificate of Release to Service (CRS)

| Point | One-line summary |
|---|---|
| M.A.801 Aircraft CRS | No aircraft is released without a CRS once all ordered maintenance has been properly done. It is issued by Subpart F or Part-CAO certifying staff, or for non-complex tasks (App. VII) by independent certifying staff (Art. 5) or the pilot-owner (M.A.803). Also covers: a 3-year-experience derogation when grounded away from base, minimum CRS content, CRS with incomplete maintenance, and no CRS if a known non-compliance endangers flight safety. Part-145 releases are out of scope. |
| M.A.802 Component CRS | A CRS is required at the end of component maintenance under M.A.502. EASA Form 1 is the component CRS, except for work under M.A.502(b) or (d), which falls under aircraft release (M.A.801). Part-145 releases and M.A.502(e) cases are excluded. |
| M.A.803 Pilot-owner authorisation | Who qualifies as a pilot-owner (valid pilot licence plus sole or joint ownership, or member of a non-profit recreational entity). Eligible aircraft are non-complex, 2 730 kg MTOM and below, and not used for CAT, commercial specialised operations or commercial ATO/DTO operations. Scope is limited to Appendix VIII tasks and listed in the AMP. Sets the content of a pilot-owner CRS. |

### Subpart I — Airworthiness Review Certificate (ARC)

| Point | One-line summary |
|---|---|
| M.A.901 Aircraft airworthiness review | Periodic review of the aircraft and its records keeps the airworthiness certificate valid. The ARC (Form 15a/15b) is valid for 1 year. Covers: definition of a controlled environment; who may issue or extend (CAMO/CAO or competent authority, by aircraft category); up to 2 extensions of 1 year; the documented review checklist (k); physical survey (l)(m); 90-day anticipation; issue only by authorised ARS after a complete review; copy to the State of registry within 10 days; no subcontracting; inconclusive review reported within 72 hours; no ARC until all findings are closed. |
| M.A.902 Validity of the ARC | When an ARC becomes invalid (ARC, CofA or TC suspended or revoked; aircraft off a Member State register). When the aircraft must not fly. Return of the ARC on surrender or revocation. |
| M.A.903 Transfer of registration within the Union | The applicant informs the former State, then applies to the new State for a new airworthiness certificate. The old ARC stays valid until its expiry date. |
| M.A.904 Airworthiness review of imported aircraft | Import from a third country or a non-2018/1139 regime needs: an application for a new airworthiness certificate, an airworthiness review (if not new), and maintenance to the approved AMP. The reviewing organisation sends a recommendation. The authority issues the CofA and the ARC (1 year, unless reduced for safety). |
| M.A.905 Findings | Defines level 1 and level 2 findings. (Chunk ends at (b).) |

## 2. Obligations

### M.A.801 Aircraft CRS

- **Who issues (b):** authorised certifying staff of a Part-M Subpart F maintenance organisation or a Part-CAO organisation. For maintenance tasks **other than complex maintenance tasks listed in Appendix VII**, the CRS may instead be issued by:
  1. independent certifying staff acting under **Article 5** of the Regulation;
  2. the **pilot-owner** acting under M.A.803.
- **When (b):** before flight. No aircraft may be released "unless a CRS is issued when all maintenance tasks ordered have been properly carried out". AMC M.A.801(b) says a CRS is needed "before flight, at the completion of any defect rectification", including between scheduled checks.
- **Out of scope (a):** aircraft released by a Part-145 organisation (covered by 145.A.50).
- **Assistants (d):** for an independent certifying staff release under (b)(1), helpers may do tasks only under the certifier's "direct and continuous control".
- **Aircraft grounded away from base (c), the derogation:** it applies only in unforeseen situations where no Part-M Subpart F, Part-145 or Part-CAO organisation and no independent certifying staff are available. The owner may then authorise any person with:
  - "no less than 3 years of appropriate maintenance experience", **and**
  - either a valid ICAO Annex 1 compliant maintenance licence for the aircraft type, or a certifying staff authorisation valid for the work, issued by an ICAO Annex 6 approved maintenance organisation.

  That person maintains the aircraft to Subpart D standards and releases it. The owner must then:
  1. obtain and keep in the aircraft records the details of the maintenance done and the qualifications of the person who issued the CRS;
  2. ensure the work is verified and a **new CRS** is issued by a person under (b) or by a Subpart F, Part-145 or Part-CAO organisation "at the earliest opportunity and in any case **within 7 calendar days** from the issuance of a CRS by the person authorised by the owner";
  3. notify the CAMO/CAO, if one is contracted, or otherwise the competent authority, "**within 7 days** from the issuance of such authorisation". Note the different trigger: point 2 runs from the CRS, point 3 runs from the authorisation.
- **Incomplete maintenance (f):** if the required maintenance cannot be completed, a CRS may be issued within the approved aircraft limitations. The CRS must say that the maintenance was not completed and state any airworthiness or operational limitations under (e)(4).
- **Prohibition (g):** no CRS may be issued for any known non-compliance that endangers flight safety.

### M.A.802 Component CRS

- A CRS must be issued at the completion of any maintenance on a component under M.A.502. Exclusions: Part-145 releases and M.A.502(e) cases.
- EASA Form 1 is the component CRS. Exception: work done under M.A.502(b) or (d) is released through the aircraft CRS process (M.A.801).

### M.A.803 Pilot-owner

- **Qualification (a):**
  1. holds a valid pilot licence (or equivalent) issued or validated by a Member State for the type or class rating; **and**
  2. owns the aircraft, solely or jointly, as either (i) one of the natural persons on the registration form, or (ii) a member of a non-profit recreational legal entity named on the registration document as owner or operator. That member must be directly involved in the entity's decision-making and designated by it to do pilot-owner maintenance.
- **Eligible aircraft (b):** "any other than complex motor-powered aircraft of 2 730 kg MTOM and below". Excluded: aircraft used in CAT, commercial specialised operations, or commercial operations by ATOs or DTOs.
- **Scope (b)(c):** limited pilot-owner maintenance as in **Appendix VIII**. The scope must be specified in the AMP (M.A.302).
- **CRS (d):** entered in the aircraft continuing airworthiness record system (see section 3).
- AMC: a pilot-owner may issue a CRS only for maintenance he or she has performed. Lack of a valid medical does **not** invalidate the licence for this purpose.

### M.A.901 Airworthiness review and ARC

- **Periodicity:** the review is carried out periodically to keep the airworthiness certificate valid. **The ARC "shall be valid for 1 year"** (a).
- **Controlled environment (b):** during "the preceding 12 months" the aircraft has:
  1. had its airworthiness continuously managed by a unique CAMO or CAO; **and**
  2. been maintained by a Part-M Subpart F, Part-145 or Part-CAO organisation. Pilot-owner tasks under M.A.803(b), released under M.A.801(b)(1) or (b)(2), are included.

**Who may issue and extend:**

| Aircraft category | Controlled environment? | Issuer | Extensions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Used by air carriers licensed under Reg. 1008/2008 (any mass), or above 2 730 kg MTOM | Yes | The managing CAMO/CAO, under its privilege: CAMO.A.125(e), M.A.711(b) or CAO.A.095(c)(1) (c)(1) | "at most twice", "for a period of 1 year each time", if it stays in a controlled environment (c)(2) |
| Same category | No, **or** the managing organisation lacks the ARC privilege | **Competent authority**, on a CAMO/CAO recommendation (Form 15a) sent with the owner's or operator's application. The recommendation must rest on an M.A.901 review (d). | n/a (authority-issued) |
| 2 730 kg MTOM and below, not used by 1008/2008 air carriers | Either | **Any** CAMO or CAO chosen by the owner or operator, with the privilege (e)(1) | at most twice, 1 year each time, if it stays in a controlled environment **under its management** (e)(2) |
| Any aircraft in a controlled environment where the ARC was issued by the authority or another CAMO/CAO | Yes | n/a | The managing organisation may extend "at most twice … for a period of 1 year each time" (f) |

- Every issue or extension is subject to (j): **no issue or extension "if there is evidence or indications that the aircraft is not airworthy"**.
- **Authority must do the review (g):** where there is a potential risk to aviation safety.
- **Authority may do the review (h):** (1) where the CAMO/CAO has its principal place of business in a third country; (2) for any other aircraft of 2 730 kg MTOM and below, at the owner's request.
- **Owner support duties (i):** when the authority does the review or assesses a recommendation, the owner or operator must provide the documentation required, suitable accommodation at the appropriate location, and the support of certifying staff.
- **Content of the review:** a full documented review (k, 11 items) plus a physical survey (l)(m). ARS not qualified under Part-66 must be assisted by qualified staff (l).
- **Anticipation (n):** the review "may be anticipated by a maximum period of **90 days** without loss of continuity of the airworthiness review pattern", so the physical survey can fall in a maintenance check.
- **Who signs (o):** Form 15b (ARC) or Form 15a (recommendation) only by authorised ARS on behalf of the approved organisation, and only once the review is completely carried out.
- **Notification (p):** a copy of any ARC issued **or extended** goes to the Member State of registry "**within 10 days**".
- **No subcontracting (q):** airworthiness review tasks "shall not be subcontracted".
- **Inconclusive review (r):** the organisation informs the competent authority "as soon as possible and in any case **within 72 hours** from the moment the organisation identifies the reason" the review is inconclusive.
- **Findings (s):** no ARC until **all findings have been closed**.

### AMC timing rules on extensions (AMC M.A.901(c)2, (e)2, (f))

- An extension needs no new review, only verification of continuous compliance with M.A.901(b).
- An extension may be anticipated by **up to 30 days**; the new expiry is still **1 year after the previous expiry**. The 12-month controlled-environment look-back also shrinks by up to 30 days.
- An extension may be done **after expiry** if all conditions are met. In that case the aircraft **cannot fly** between expiry and extension, and the new expiry is 1 year after the **previous expiry**, not 1 year after the extension date.

### M.A.902 Validity

- **ARC invalid (a)** if: (1) suspended or revoked; (2) the airworthiness certificate is suspended or revoked; (3) the aircraft is not on a Member State register; (4) the TC under which the airworthiness certificate was issued is suspended or revoked.
- **Aircraft must not fly (b)** if the airworthiness certificate is invalid, or: (1) the continuing airworthiness of the aircraft or any fitted component does not meet Part-M; (2) the aircraft no longer conforms to the type design approved by the Agency; (3) it was operated beyond AFM or airworthiness certificate limits without appropriate action; (4) an accident or incident affected airworthiness without action to restore it; (5) a modification or repair does not comply with M.A.304.
- **(c):** on surrender or revocation, the ARC is returned to the competent authority.

### M.A.903 Transfer within the Union

- The applicant (1) informs the former Member State which State the aircraft will be registered in, **then** (2) applies to the new State for a new airworthiness certificate under Part 21 or Part 21 Light.
- (b): notwithstanding M.A.902(a)(3), the **former ARC remains valid until its expiry date**.
- AMC: notify the former State's authority so information can pass between authorities. The owner or operator should check that the new State's authority has entered the new registration on the existing ARC and validated the change.
- UK guess: this point is probably deleted or reworded for the UK. A transfer from an EU register to G-registry is an import under the UK equivalent of M.A.904.

### M.A.904 Import

- **The applicant (a) must:**
  1. apply to the State of registry for a new airworthiness certificate (Part 21 or Part 21 Light);
  2. for aircraft other than new, have an M.A.901 airworthiness review done;
  3. have all maintenance done to comply with the M.A.302-approved AMP.
- **(b):** the reviewing organisation sends a documented recommendation to the State of registry.
- **(c):** the owner allows the authority access to inspect.
- **(d)(e):** the authority issues the airworthiness certificate and the ARC. The ARC is "valid for 1 year, unless the competent authority decides to reduce the period of validity for reasons of aviation safety".
- **AMC notice periods:** inform the authority of the time and location of the import review "**at least 10 working days in advance**" (AMC M.A.904(a)1). If no CAMO or maintenance organisation is approved for the type, the review can be requested from the authority "with a **30-day notice**" (AMC M.A.904(a)2 para 3).

### M.A.905 Findings

- **Level 1:** "any finding of significant non-compliance with the requirements of this Annex, which lowers the safety standard and seriously endangers flight safety".
- **Level 2:** "any finding of non-compliance with the requirements of this Annex, which may lower the safety standard and may endanger the flight safety".

## 3. Records and Documents

### Aircraft CRS (M.A.801(e) and AMC M.A.801(e))

**Minimum content:**
1. basic details of the maintenance carried out;
2. the date the maintenance was completed;
3. the identity of the issuer: (i) the maintenance organisation's approval reference and the certifying staff member, **or** (ii) for a pilot-owner release under (b)(2), the identity and, where applicable, the licence number;
4. limitations to airworthiness or operations, if any. For incomplete maintenance this includes the "not completed" statement.

**AMC standard statements:**
- General: "Certifies that the work specified except as otherwise specified was carried out in accordance with Part-M and in respect to that work the aircraft is considered ready for release to service".
- Pilot-owner: "Certifies that the limited pilot-owner maintenance specified except as otherwise specified was carried out in accordance with Part-M and in respect to that work the aircraft is considered ready for release to service".

**Other AMC content rules:**
- Reference the task in the manufacturer's or operator's instruction or the AMP (which may cross-refer to the AMM, SBs and so on).
- The "date" should place the work relative to life or overhaul limits (date, flying hours, cycles, landings).
- Extensive maintenance may be summarised with a **unique cross-reference to the work-pack**. Dimensional information stays in the work-pack.
- **Signature:** normal signature. An electronic release is allowed if the authority is satisfied that only that person can issue it (for example a personal magnetic or optical card plus a PIN). A certification stamp is optional.
- At completion, all parties should have a clear, concise, legible record of the work.
- **Independent certifying staff (M.A.801(b)1)** retain all records needed to prove that the CRS requirements were met.

### Owner-authorised release (M.A.801(c))

The owner keeps in the aircraft records:
- details of the maintenance;
- the qualifications of the person who issued the CRS;

plus the later verification CRS and evidence of the 7-day notification.

### Incomplete-maintenance CRS (AMC M.A.801(f))

- Endorse on the CRS the details of the deferment, including the **owner, CAO or CAMO authorisation**.
- Keep a record of what the mechanic, supervisor and certifying staff should do to bring the matter to the owner, CAO or CAMO.

### Pilot-owner CRS (M.A.803(d))

- Entered in the **aircraft continuing airworthiness record system**.
- Contents: basic details of the maintenance, **maintenance data used**, date completed, and the **identity, signature and pilot licence number** of the pilot-owner.
- If an equivalent qualification is used, its number replaces the licence number (AMC 4).

### AMP content linked to the pilot-owner (M.A.803(c), AMC 3)

- The scope of limited pilot-owner maintenance.
- For jointly owned aircraft: the names of all competent, designated pilot-owners and the tasks they may perform. Alternatively, a procedure for keeping that list current separately.

### Component CRS — EASA Form 1 (M.A.802, AMC)

- Releases components after manufacture or maintenance, so they can move from one aircraft or component to another (Form 1 template: Appendix II to Part-M).
- An organisation maintaining a component for its own use may not need a Form 1, depending on its internal procedures. All the Form 1 information must still be in the CRS.

### EASA Form 123 — Standard Change / Standard Repair embodiment record (AMC M.A.801)

**Boxes:**
1. SC/SR number(s): a unique number, referenced in the aircraft logbook.
2. Title and description: the CS-STAN chapter with revision (for example CS-SCxxxy or CS-SRxxxy), title and a short description.
3. Applicability: aircraft registration, serial number and type.
4. List of parts: description, P/N, quantity; an auxiliary document may be used.
5. Operational limitations and affected aircraft manuals. Copies go to the owner.
6. Documents used for development and embodiment (design data, compliance showing, test results), with revision or issue. Items marked * go to the owner.
7. ICA to be considered in the AMP review. Copies go to the owner.
8. Other information.
9a. SC compliance statement: complies with 21A.90B(a) and the relevant CS-STAN paragraphs.
9b. SR compliance statement: complies with 21A.431B(a) and the relevant CS-STAN paragraphs. "Relevant paragraphs" means CS-STAN Subpart A General plus the specific SC/SR.
10. Date of embodiment.
11. Identification data and signature of the person responsible (full name plus the certificate reference used for the release).
12. Owner signature, attesting handover of documentation and awareness of limitations and extra continuing airworthiness requirements. If an M.A.201 contract exists, a CAMO representative may sign and must pass the information to the owner before the next flight.

**Rules for Form 123:**
- Filled in in English or the official language of the State of registry. Form 123 Issue 00.
- The **original** stays with the person responsible for the embodiment; the **owner keeps a copy** and receives copies of the box 5 and 7 documents and the starred box 6 documents.
- The logbook entry refers to the Form 123. The **same person signs Form 123 and the CRS**.
- Controlled documentation applies: reference numbers, issue dates, revision numbers and names of preparer and releaser.
- The engineering file (drawings, parts list, analysis, tests, compliance statement, manual and ICA amendments) is scaled to complexity.
- Record-keeping by the embodier is as Part-M, Part-145 and CS-STAN require. The owner or CAMO keeps change and repair status under M.A.305.
- Several related SCs (CS-STAN Subpart B) may share one Form 123 and one CRS, with traceability to each.

### ARC — EASA Form 15a / 15b (Appendix III)

- Per M.A.901(o) and GM M.A.901(a): **Form 15b** is the ARC issued by a CAMO or CAO. **Form 15a** is the ARC issued by competent authorities.
- Note the ambiguity in the text: point (o) calls Form 15a "the recommendation for the issue of the airworthiness review certificate". The software should treat 15a as the authority-issued ARC and model the CAMO/CAO recommendation as a separate document.
- Signed by authorised ARS on behalf of the organisation. A copy goes to the State of registry within 10 days of issue or extension.
- Returned to the authority on surrender or revocation.
- (Form 15c for Part-ML is outside this chunk.)

### Compliance reports (GM M.A.901, AMC M.A.901(k), (l)(m), (o))

- The ARS produce a compliance report detailing all items checked and the outcome, covering both the documented review and the physical survey.
- A copy of both reports goes to the authority with any recommendation.

### Full documented review: document categories to check (AMC M.A.901(k))

Sample checks are the minimum within each category:
- registration papers;
- the M.A.305 continuing airworthiness record system;
- the M.A.306 technical log;
- list of deferred defects, MEL and CDL (if applicable);
- AFM including configuration;
- AMP;
- maintenance data;
- relevant work packages;
- AD status;
- modification and SB status;
- modification and repair approval sheets;
- LLP and time-controlled component status;
- relevant EASA Form 1s or equivalent;
- mass and balance report and equipment list;
- aircraft, engine and propeller TCDS or airworthiness data sheet.

### Recommendation to the authority (AMC M.A.901(d))

- **Language:** at least in English if the State of registry differs from the CAMO/CAO's State; otherwise the CAMO/CAO's official language.
- **Contents:**
  - (a) general information: CAMO, owner or lessee, date and place of the document review and survey, period and place the aircraft can be seen;
  - (b) aircraft: registration, type, manufacturer, serial number, AFM reference, weight and CG data, AMP reference;
  - (c) attachments: copy of the registration papers and the owner's request for a new ARC;
  - (d) status: total time and cycles, and a list of the persons or organisations that did continuing airworthiness activities (including maintenance) on the aircraft and its components since the last ARC;
  - (e) survey: the precise areas surveyed and their status;
  - (f) all findings, with the corrective action taken;
  - (g) a signed ARS statement recommending issue.
- The statement in (g) confirms compliance of the current configuration with: ADs up to the latest issue, the TCDS or airworthiness data sheet, the AMP, LLP and time-controlled component limits, a valid weight and CG schedule, Part 21 or Part 21 Light for all modifications and repairs, the current AFM and supplements, and operational requirements.
- Each item gives the **exact reference of the data used** (for example the TCDS number and issue). It also confirms that everything is entered in the continuing airworthiness record system and/or the technical log.

### Import recommendation (AMC M.A.904(b))

- Everything in AMC M.A.901(d), plus:
  - **aircraft information:** assigned registration, State of manufacturer, previous registration, export certificate number, TC and TCDS numbers, noise and emissions TCDS numbers, and a comparison of the old and new AMP;
  - **attachments:** copy of the application, **original** export certificate, AFM and supplement approvals, list of ADs incorporated to the latest issue, proposed AMP, LLP and time-controlled component status, valid weight and CG schedule, and the Part 21 or Part 21 Light approval or declaration reference for every modification and repair;
  - copies of the work packages, including any bridging check;
  - a copy of the maintenance check flight report.

### Retention periods

None are stated in this chunk. Record retention is in M.A.305 and M.A.306 and in the organisation subparts (Subpart F, Part-CAMO, Part-CAO, Part-145), which are in other chunks.

## 4. Defined Terms

- **CRS (certificate of release to service):** the certificate issued, when all ordered maintenance has been properly carried out, before an aircraft is released. For components it is EASA Form 1 (M.A.802).
- **Pilot-owner:** a valid pilot licence (or equivalent) holder who owns the aircraft solely or jointly, as a natural person on the registration form or a designated, decision-involved member of a non-profit recreational legal entity named as owner or operator (M.A.803(a)).
- **Limited pilot-owner maintenance:** the tasks in Appendix VIII, with scope defined in the AMP.
- **"Privately operated"** (AMC M.A.803): "the aircraft is operated pursuant to M.A.201(i)".
- **Equivalent valid pilot licence** (AMC M.A.803): any document attesting a pilot qualification recognised by the Member State and issued under its system, not necessarily by the competent authority.
- **"3 years of appropriate maintenance experience"** (AMC M.A.801(c)): "3 years working in an aircraft maintenance environment on at least some of the aircraft type systems corresponding to the aircraft endorsed on the aircraft maintenance license or on the certifying staff authorisation that the person holds".
- **"Holding the proper qualifications"** (AMC M.A.801(c)): a valid ICAO Annex 1 compliant licence for the type, or a certifying staff authorisation from an ICAO Annex 6 approved maintenance organisation valid for the work.
- **Incomplete maintenance** (AMC M.A.801(f)): ordered maintenance not completed because maintenance downtime ran out, or because the maintenance data requires a flight as part of the maintenance.
- **"Endangers flight safety"** (AMC M.A.801(g)): "any instance where safe operation could not be assured or which could lead to an unsafe condition". Examples: significant cracking, deformation, corrosion or failure of primary structure; burning; electrical arcing; significant hydraulic or fuel leakage; any emergency system or total system failure. **"An AD overdue for compliance is also considered a hazard to flight safety."**
- **Eligible installer (SC/SR)** (AMC M.A.801): only persons entitled to release an aircraft after maintenance under Part-M, Part-145 or Part-CAO.
- **Standard Change / Standard Repair (SC/SR):** a change or repair under CS-STAN that needs no specific design approval (criteria 21A.90B(a) and 21A.431B(a)).
- **Controlled environment** (M.A.901(b)): during the preceding 12 months, airworthiness continuously managed by a unique CAMO or CAO **and** maintained by Subpart F, Part-145 or Part-CAO organisations (pilot-owner releases included). Per AMC M.A.901(b), without an Appendix I continuing airworthiness contract the aircraft is outside a controlled environment, unless operator and CAMO are the same organisation. Unreleased pilot-owner maintenance does not break the controlled environment if the contracted CAMO was informed.
- **Airworthiness review certificate (ARC):** Form 15a (authority) or Form 15b (CAMO/CAO). It certifies airworthiness only within the scope of the review and **at the time of the review**, and that the ARS know of no non-compliance endangering flight safety (GM M.A.901).
- **Airworthiness review staff (ARS):** authorised staff of the approved organisation who perform both the documented review and the physical survey and sign the ARC or recommendation.
- **Full documented review** (AMC M.A.901(k)): a check of at least the listed document categories, with sample checks as the minimum.
- **Physical survey** (M.A.901(l)(m)): ARS checks of markings and placards, AFM compliance, configuration against approved documentation, no unaddressed evident defects, and no inconsistency with the records review.
- **"Without loss of continuity of the airworthiness review pattern"** (AMC M.A.901(n)): "the new expiration date is set up 1 year after the previous expiration date". So an anticipated ARC can be valid up to 90 days longer than 1 year.
- **Inconclusive review** (M.A.901(r)): a review whose outcome cannot be determined (the term itself is not defined further).
- **Level 1 finding / Level 2 finding:** see M.A.905 in section 2.
- **Complex maintenance tasks:** those listed in Appendix VII (the list itself is not in this chunk).
- **Independent certifying staff:** certifying staff acting under Article 5 of the Regulation, not on behalf of an organisation.

## 5. Software Implications

### Data objects and fields

**ReleaseToService (aircraft CRS)**
- Fields: id; aircraft; work order or work-pack reference (unique cross-reference); summary of the maintenance; task references (AMP task, AMM, SB, AD); maintenance data used, with revision; completion date; aircraft hours, cycles and landings at completion.
- Issuer type: Subpart F or Part-CAO organisation certifying staff / independent certifying staff (Art. 5) / pilot-owner / owner-authorised person (M.A.801(c)).
- Issuer details: organisation approval reference; certifier name and authorisation or licence number; pilot licence number or equivalent qualification number.
- Statement text: standard Part-M wording, or the pilot-owner variant.
- Limitations: free text plus structured entries.
- Completion status: complete / incomplete. If incomplete: reason (downtime or flight required), deferment authorisation (who in the owner, CAO or CAMO agreed, and when), endorsed on the CRS.
- Signature method: wet / electronic (personal credential plus PIN); optional stamp.
- Status: draft / signed / superseded (for example by the verification CRS after M.A.801(c)).

**OwnerAuthorisedRelease (M.A.801(c))**
- Fields: location; reason no approved organisation was available; person name; years of experience (at least 3); ICAO Annex 1 licence or ICAO Annex 6 organisation authorisation details; date of authorisation; date of CRS.
- Links: `verification_CRS`; `notification_sent_at` and recipient (CAMO/CAO or authority).
- Deadlines: verification CRS due = CRS date + 7 calendar days; notification due = authorisation date + 7 days.

**ComponentRelease**
- Fields: EASA/CAA Form 1 reference; whether it is a Form 1 or an internal release (with all Form 1 data fields); the M.A.502 paragraph the work falls under. (b)/(d) work goes to the aircraft CRS flow.

**PilotOwner**
- Fields: person; licence (number, issuing or validating State, type/class rating, valid flag); equivalent qualification flag; ownership basis (registered natural person, or member of a recreational entity with a designation document); aircraft links.
- Medical status is **not** an eligibility gate.

**AMP extension for the pilot-owner**
- The authorised Appendix VIII task list.
- For joint ownership, the designated pilot-owners and their tasks (or a reference to the managed list procedure).

**SC/SR embodiment (Form 123)**
- Model all 12 boxes as fields.
- Also: unique SC/SR number linked to a logbook entry; CS-STAN reference with revision; parts list; affected manuals; supporting documents with revision and a "handed to owner" flag; ICA links that trigger an AMP review task; SC or SR compliance statement; embodiment date; signer (must equal the CRS signer).
- Owner or CAMO countersignature (box 12), with a "provided to owner before next flight" check where the CAMO signs.
- Multi-SC grouping.
- Feeds the M.A.305 modification and repair status.

**AirworthinessReview**
- Fields: aircraft; organisation; ARS (authorised staff id); type (ARC issue / recommendation to authority / import); planned and actual date of the documented review; physical survey date and location; anticipation days relative to ARC expiry (at most 90).
- Records-review checklist: the M.A.901(k) 11 items plus the AMC categories, each with sample evidence and result.
- Physical-survey checklist: the M.A.901(m) 5 items, the areas surveyed and their status, any maintenance raised (generates a CRS), and any in-flight checks.
- Part-66 assistant(s): they perform and release maintenance only, not the survey.
- Findings list. Compliance reports (documented and physical).
- Outcome: satisfactory / unsatisfactory / inconclusive. If inconclusive, `reason_identified_at` and `authority_notified_at`, due within 72 h.

**Finding**
- Fields: level (1 or 2, M.A.905); description; requirement reference; corrective action; closed date and evidence.
- Gate: the ARC cannot be issued while any finding is open.

**ARC**
- Fields: form (15a authority / 15b CAMO-CAO; Part-ML 15c elsewhere); ARC reference; aircraft registration (updatable on in-Union transfer, with an authority validation flag); issuer (organisation approval reference or authority); ARS signatory; issue date; expiry date.
- Extension history: up to 2 entries, each with date, signatory, extending organisation, and controlled-environment check evidence.
- `copy_sent_to_registry_state_at` (due within 10 days of issue or extension).
- Status: valid / expired / suspended / revoked / invalid (derived) / surrendered or returned.

**ControlledEnvironmentStatus (derived per aircraft)**
- Inputs: CAMO/CAO management history (a single unique organisation over the window); continuing airworthiness contract (Appendix I) or operator = CAMO; maintenance events, each with organisation type (Subpart F, Part-145, Part-CAO, pilot-owner release, independent certifying staff, owner-authorised person).
- Pilot-owner work counts if the CAMO was informed.
- The look-back is 12 months from the last ARC issue or extension, which may be 30 days shorter for extension anticipation or 90 days shorter for review anticipation.

**Recommendation (to authority)**
- All AMC M.A.901(d) fields, plus the M.A.904(b) import fields.
- Language field (English required if the State of registry differs from the CAMO/CAO's State).
- Attached compliance reports; owner application; data references with issue numbers (TCDS and others).

**ImportCase**
- Fields: previous register and State; export certificate (original held); new registration; TCDS and noise/emissions TCDS; old versus new AMP comparison; bridging check work packages; MCF report; AD list; LLP status; EPA parts; authority-notified date (at least 10 working days before the review); authority-requested review (30-day notice).
- ARC validity may be set to less than 1 year by the authority.

**RegistrationTransfer (in-Union)**
- Fields: former State informed (date); new CofA application; ARC carried over until expiry; new registration endorsed on the ARC by the new authority.

### Business rules, validations and alerts

**Release eligibility**
- Block a CRS by independent certifying staff or a pilot-owner when any task is an Appendix VII complex maintenance task.
- Block a pilot-owner CRS when the aircraft is complex motor-powered, above 2 730 kg MTOM, or its use is CAT, commercial specialised operations or commercial ATO/DTO.
- Also block it when the task is outside the AMP's pilot-owner list, or when the pilot-owner did not personally do the work.
- Block any CRS while a known non-compliance endangering flight safety exists. Treat an **overdue AD** as automatically blocking.

**Airworthy-to-fly status**
- Aircraft not airworthy if any of these holds: no CRS after the latest maintenance; ARC expired or invalid; CofA invalid; any M.A.902(b) condition; an owner-authorised CRS not verified within 7 calendar days.
- Flag such aircraft as must-not-fly.

**ARC scheduling**
- Expiry = issue + 1 year. Extension expiry = previous expiry + 1 year, whether anticipated by up to 30 days or done late.
- If the review is done within 90 days before expiry, the new expiry = old expiry + 1 year.
- Alerts: at 90 days (review window opens), 30 days (extension window opens), and at expiry (ground the aircraft).

**ARC extension**
- Allow an extension only if: fewer than 2 extensions since the last full review; controlled environment continuously maintained; the extending organisation is the managing CAMO/CAO (per the (c)/(e)/(f) rules); no evidence the aircraft is unairworthy.
- After 2 extensions, a full airworthiness review is required.

**Issuer routing**
- Decide the issuer from: MTOM above 2 730 kg or a 1008/2008 air carrier user; controlled environment; whether the managing organisation holds the ARC privilege; whether the CAMO/CAO is in a third country (authority may review); owner request (aircraft of 2 730 kg MTOM and below, authority may review).
- Outcomes: organisation-issued Form 15b, or authority-issued Form 15a via the recommendation workflow.

**Other deadlines**
- Copy of the ARC to the State of registry within 10 days of issue or extension.
- Inconclusive review: notify the authority within 72 h.
- Import: notify the authority at least 10 working days before the review; ask the authority for a review with 30 days' notice.

**Separation of duties and permissions**
- Only authorised ARS can sign an ARC or recommendation, and the same ARS must have done both the documented review and the physical survey.
- No subcontracting: the ARS must belong to the issuing organisation.
- Part-66 assistants can only sign the maintenance CRS for survey-generated tasks.
- ARS can override sampling depth (as the GM allows) and record the reason.

**ARC lifecycle**
- ARC invalid on: ARC, CofA or TC suspension or revocation; deregistration from a Member State register.
- Exception: in-Union transfer keeps the ARC valid until expiry (UK: probably not applicable).
- On surrender or revocation, track return to the authority.

**SC/SR**
- Enforce the same signer on Form 123 and the CRS, and a logbook entry referencing the Form 123 number.
- Create an AMP review task when box 7 lists ICA.
- Track owner receipt of the documents.

### Roles and permissions to model

- **Owner:** can authorise an M.A.801(c) person, agree incomplete-maintenance deferments, sign Form 123 box 12, request an authority ARC, and provide accommodation and support.
- **Pilot-owner:** can create CRSs limited to AMP pilot-owner tasks.
- **Independent certifying staff:** non-complex tasks only; retains proof records.
- **Organisation certifying staff:** Subpart F or Part-CAO.
- **CAMO/CAO staff:** can authorise deferments and sign Form 123 box 12 under a contract.
- **ARS:** issue, extend and recommend.
- **Part-66 assistant.**
- **Competent authority:** read and receive notifications.

## 7. Notable AMC and GM

**CRS and SC/SR (AMC M.A.801)**
- Only persons entitled to release after maintenance (Part-M, Part-145, Part-CAO) can embody an SC/SR.
- For aircraft with no applicable Part-66 licence, a national certifying qualification is needed. If the holder signs for an approved maintenance organisation, it is valid for aircraft of any Member State. If the holder signs as an independent certifier, it is valid only for aircraft registered in the State that issued the qualification.
- CS-STAN may restrict who can release certain SCs or SRs.
- The releaser takes full design and embodiment responsibility.
- Form 123 is the acceptable record.
- UK guess: probably UK CS-STAN and a CAA form, and "Member State" scoping becomes UK-only.

**When a CRS is needed (AMC M.A.801(b))**
- A CRS is needed after **any defect rectification**, even between scheduled checks.

**Owner-authorised release and controlled environment (AMC M.A.801(c)3)**
- An owner-authorised release does **not** break the controlled environment, provided an approved maintenance organisation does the M.A.801(c)2 recheck and release.

**CRS content and form (AMC M.A.801(e))**
- Standard CRS statements.
- Life-limit-relative dating.
- Work-pack summarising with dimensional data kept in the work-pack.
- Electronic signature acceptance (personal card plus PIN) supports e-CRS in the app. Authority acceptance is needed.

**Incomplete maintenance (AMC M.A.801(f))**
- The owner, CAO or CAMO must be informed and **agree** to the deferment, and the authorisation is endorsed on the CRS.
- For tasks that require a flight (for example an AMM in-flight check), issue an incomplete-maintenance release before the flight, then a full CRS after the flight and any further maintenance.
- This links to MCF responsibility (GM M.A.301(i)).

**Endangers flight safety (AMC M.A.801(g))**
- The definition includes **an overdue AD**. This is a hard block on release.

**Component release (AMC M.A.802)**
- A Form 1 may not be needed for in-house component maintenance if the CRS carries all the Form 1 data.

**Pilot-owner (AMC M.A.803)**
- The pilot-owner certifies only their own work.
- For joint ownership, designated pilot-owners are listed in the AMP.
- An equivalent qualification number may replace the licence number.
- **The medical is not required.**
- UK guess: the UK pilot-owner regime is likely the 2019/1383 version. The UK may also have its own light-aircraft schemes (for example LAMP or the CAA "self-declared" programme) that affect scope.

**ARS responsibilities (GM M.A.901)**
- ARS decide sampling depth and may exceed the CAME/CAE.
- The organisation must not pressure ARS.
- A compliance report is mandatory.
- ARS are not responsible for issues not detected, or for AMP gaps relative to the design approval holder.
- The ARC is valid "at the time of the review" only. The owner or CAMO/CAO keeps ongoing responsibility.

**Form 15a vs 15b (GM M.A.901(a))**
- 15a is issued by the authority; 15b by the CAMO or CAO.

**Controlled environment (AMC M.A.901(b))**
- Needs an Appendix I contract, unless operator = CAMO.
- Pilot-owner maintenance is fine if the CAMO is informed.

**Extensions (AMC M.A.901(c)2, (e)2, (f))**
- An extension is a verification only, not a review.
- 30-day anticipation.
- A late extension is allowed, but the aircraft is grounded between expiry and extension and the expiry date is not reset.

**Recommendation (AMC M.A.901(d))**
- Detailed content list and language rule. This is a good template for an export feature.

**Accommodation (AMC M.A.901(i))**
- An office with normal equipment, and a hangar when needed.
- Part-66 support where the authority's ARS are not qualified.

**Full documented review (AMC M.A.901(k))**
- Document categories with a minimum of sample checks per category, plus a compliance report.

**Physical survey (AMC M.A.901(l)(m))**
- Survey actions that are maintenance (operational tests, panel opening) need a CRS afterwards.
- Part-66 helpers only perform and release that maintenance; the signing ARS must do the survey personally.
- The survey may include in-flight checks.
- A compliance report is needed.

**Anticipation (AMC M.A.901(n))**
- With 90-day anticipation the ARC can be valid for up to 1 year plus 90 days.
- The controlled-environment window shrinks accordingly.

**Reports to the authority (AMC M.A.901(o))**
- Send both compliance reports with any recommendation.

**Transfer (AMC M.A.903)**
- Notify the former authority.
- Check that the new authority has re-endorsed the registration on the ARC.

**Import (AMC M.A.904)**
- 10 working days' notice to the authority.
- A detailed list of what to review: export certificate, history, logbooks, AD and SB status, LLPs, modifications including STCs and EPA parts, cabin and emergency equipment, avionics (including DFDR/CVR tests and ELT 406 MHz coding), compass compensation, special operations approvals (ETOPS/LROPS, RVSM, MNPS, AWOPS, RNAV), the survey, and the MCF.
- 30-day notice for an authority-performed review where no approved organisation exists.
- A full content list for the import recommendation, including the **original export certificate** and the AMP comparison or bridging check.
