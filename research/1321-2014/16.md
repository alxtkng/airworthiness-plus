# PART-147 — Maintenance Training Organisations

## 1. Scope (point by point)

General
- **147.1** Competent authority: Member State-designated authority for organisations with principal place of business in a Member State; EASA for those in a third country. (AMC: may be a ministry/NAA/other body; multiple CAs allowed if one per area of responsibility.)

Section A, Subpart A — General
- **147.A.05 Scope**: requirements for organisations seeking approval to conduct Part-66 training and examination.
- **147.A.10 General**: training organisation must be an organisation (or part of one) registered as a legal entity. (GM: may operate from several addresses and hold several Part approvals.)
- **147.A.15 Application**: application for approval/change on CA form; must include registered name and address, address(es) requiring approval, intended scope/change of scope, accountable manager name and signature, date. (AMC: content per EASA Form 12.)

Subpart B — Organisational requirements
- **147.A.100 Facility requirements**: weather-protected facilities; enclosed separate theory/exam accommodation free from distraction; basic-course workshops/maintenance facilities separate from classrooms (or by written agreement with another organisation, giving CA access); type training access to aircraft type facilities; max 15 students per instructor/assessor in practical training; office accommodation for instructors/examiners/assessors; secure storage for exam/training records; library of technical material; (j) distance-learning derogation from (a)-(d),(f) where organisation has no control of the student's location - must brief student on suitability of location; derogation does not apply to the exam/assessment.
- **147.A.105 Personnel requirements**: accountable manager with corporate authority to finance training; nominated compliance person(s) responsible to AM (may be AM); sufficient contracted staff for training/exams/assessments; other organisation's staff may do practical training/assessment when that organisation provides it; roles of instructor/examiner/assessor may be combined; qualifications per CA-agreed criteria; examiners and assessors specified in exposition; instructors and knowledge examiners updating training at least every 24 months.
- **147.A.110 Records of instructors, examiners and assessors**: maintain records (experience, qualification, training history, subsequent training); terms of reference for all.
- **147.A.115 Instructional equipment**: legible presentation equipment per classroom; virtual training content must be readable/designed for understanding; MSTDs allowed as presentation aids; workshops must have all tools/equipment and an appropriate selection of aircraft, engines, parts, avionics; type training must have access to the aircraft type (MSTDs allowed if adequate standard).
- **147.A.120 Maintenance training material**: course material to students covering Part-66 basic syllabus and/or type course content; access to library examples; may be hard copy or electronic, accessible at any time for the whole course.
- **147.A.125 Records**: keep all student training, examination and assessment records for an **unlimited period**.
- **147.A.130 Training procedures and quality system**: procedures acceptable to CA; quality system with independent audit function (training standards, exam/assessment integrity, procedures) and feedback system of findings to the AM for corrective action.
- **147.A.135 Examinations**: question security; cheating students disqualified and barred 12 months, CA informed within 1 calendar month; examiners supplying answers disqualified, exam void, CA informed within 1 calendar month; exams in a "controlled environment" by the approved organisation, described in the MTOE.
- **147.A.140 Maintenance training organisation exposition (MTOE)**: 12 required content items (see Records section); CA approves MTOE and amendments; minor amendments by indirect approval procedure.
- **147.A.145 Privileges**: basic courses (whole or part), type/task courses, exams of own students, exams of students who did not attend type course, exams of students who did not attend basic course (at approved locations, or elsewhere only via ECQB or CA-selected questions), issue of certificates (Forms 148/149); locations limited to those on certificate or in MTOE, with derogation via MTOE control procedure; subcontracting to non-147 organisations under quality system control, basic limited to Modules 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10; type limited to powerplant and avionics; no exam approval without corresponding training approval, except type examination where type training is not required.
- **147.A.150 Changes**: notify CA of proposed changes affecting approval before they happen; CA may set operating conditions or suspend; failure to notify may result in suspension/revocation backdated to date of change.
- **147.A.155 Continued validity**: unlimited duration, subject to compliance (findings per 147.B.130), CA access, not surrendered/revoked; certificate returned on surrender/revocation.
- **147.A.160 Findings**: level 1 = significant exam non-compliance invalidating exams, refusing CA access after two written requests, no AM, significant training non-compliance; level 2 = any other training non-compliance; holder defines corrective action plan and demonstrates action within CA-agreed period.

Subpart C — Approved basic training course
- **147.A.200 Approved basic training course**: knowledge training + knowledge exam + practical training + practical assessment; knowledge covers Part-66 (sub)category syllabus; exam covers representative cross-section; practical covers tooling, disassembly/assembly, representative maintenance per complete module; assessment checks tool use and working to manuals; duration per Appendix I; hours may be amended (training technology or 66.A.25(e) credits) with justification and MTOE procedure; conversion course durations by syllabus assessment.
- **147.A.205 Basic knowledge examinations**: to Part-66 standard, without training notes, representative cross-section of the module.
- **147.A.210 Basic practical assessment**: by nominated practical assessors at completion of each workshop/maintenance-facility visit period; student must achieve an assessed pass vs 147.A.200(e).

Subpart D — Aircraft type/task training
- **147.A.300 Aircraft type/task training**: approval to conduct Part-66 type and/or task training subject to 66.A.45 standard.
- **147.A.305 Aircraft type evaluation and task assessment**: an organisation approved for type training conducts the Part-66 type evaluation / task assessment to 66.A.45.

Section B — Procedures for competent authorities
- **147.B.05 Scope**: administrative requirements for CAs.
- **147.B.10 Competent authority**: designated CA with documented procedures and structure; adequate staffing; procedures kept current; staff qualified and trained (initial and continuation) in Part-66/Part-147.
- **147.B.20 Record-keeping**: traceable record system; 8 minimum record types; retention minimum 4 years.
- **147.B.25 Exemptions**: CA may exempt a State education department school from legal-entity requirement, from having an AM (senior person with sufficient budget instead), and from independent audit (if an independent schools inspectorate audits at the required frequency); exemptions under Art. 71(1) of 2018/1139 recorded and retained.
- Subpart B intro: requirements to issue/vary approval.
- **147.B.110 Procedure for approval and changes**: CA reviews MTOE and verifies compliance; findings recorded and confirmed in writing; all findings closed before approval issued; reference number on certificate as EASA specifies.
- **147.B.120 Continued validity procedure**: complete audit at intervals not exceeding 24 months, including monitoring at least one training course and one examination; findings per 147.B.130.
- **147.B.125 Approval certificate**: format per Appendix II (EASA Form 11).
- **147.B.130 Findings**: level 1 not rectified within 3 days of written notification -> revocation/suspension/limitation in whole or part; level 2 not rectified within CA-granted timescale -> revoke/limit/suspend.
- **147.B.200 Revocation, suspension, limitation**: suspend on reasonable grounds for potential safety threat; or suspend/revoke/limit per 147.B.130.

Appendices to Annex IV
- **Appendix I**: basic training course minimum durations (table below).
- **Appendix II**: EASA Form 11 approval certificate and schedule.
- **Appendix III**: Certificates of Recognition — EASA Forms 148a/148b (basic) and 149a/149b (type).

Appendices to AMC
- Appendix I: MTOE format; Appendix II: EASA Form 4; Appendix III: EASA Form 22; Appendix IV: EASA Form 12.

## 4. Defined Terms

- **Controlled environment** (exams, 147.A.135(d)): environment where identity of students, proper conduct, integrity of the exam and security of exam material can be established and verified.
- **Indirect approval**: approval of minor MTOE amendments via an exposition procedure (147.A.140(c)).
- **Level 1 / Level 2 finding** (147.A.160).
- **Training hour**: 60 minutes of tuition excluding breaks, examination, revision, preparation and aircraft visit (AMC1 147.A.200(f)).
- **Large / small training organisation**: capacity >= 50 / < 50 students.
- **Sub-contract control system**: Part-147 approval temporarily extended to include the subcontractor for the duration; Part-147 org remains responsible.
- **Airframe type training course**: all structure and electrical/mechanical systems excluding powerplant. **Powerplant type training course**: bare engine incl. build-up to QEC unit. Engine/airframe interface covered by either (GA often in airframe). **Avionics/electrical systems type training**: ATA 22, 23, 24, 25, 27, 31, 33, 34, 42, 44, 45, 46, 73, 77 or equivalent (AMC1 147.A.300). Task training may use MBT.
- Training tools (AMC1 147.A.130(a) Table 1, numbered 1-16): slideshow; manuals; computer; mobile devices; videos; **MSTD** (maintenance simulation training device: hardware/software device for training, exam and/or assessment on a component, system or whole aircraft); **mock-up** (scaled/full replica preserving geometric, operational or functional characteristics); virtual reality; **MTD** (maintenance training device, any device other than MSTD, may include mock-ups); **real aircraft** ("suitable" = of the type, or of licence (sub)category with same equipment, for type training; representative for basic; excludes virtual aircraft; "condition" = main components fitted and systems operable); aircraft component; augmented reality; embedded training (e.g. centralised fault display); classroom; virtual classroom (simulated location for synchronous learning); virtual aircraft. **STD** synthetic training devices = generic term covering MSTD, MTD, FSTD.
- Training methods (Table 2): assisted learning (mentoring); CBT (computer-based training, not competency-based); demonstration; distance learning asynchronous / synchronous; e-learning; lecturing; M-learning; multimedia-based training (MBT); simulation; web-based training (WBT). Instructor-centred vs student-centred vs blended training defined.
- Table 3 maps each method to permitted tools and to theory levels 1-3, practical, OJT, and learning objectives (knowledge/skills/attitude), with "limited suitability" marks and "only type"/"type unlimited" notes. Distance learning and M-learning: limited suitability for practical. Instructor involvement should be considered in Module 9 (human factors).
- **ECQB**: European Central Question Bank.
- **Relevant engineering degree** (CA surveyor): aeronautical, mechanical, electrical, electronic, avionic or other relevant studies.

## 5. Software Implications (Airworthiness+)

- **Engineer licensing module**: store CoRs (Form 148a/b, 149a/b) as structured records: CoR reference (`MS.147.XXXX.YYYYY` or `MS.CAA.XXXX.YYYYY`), issuing org ref, holder name/DoB/place, form type, per-module exam pass dates and Part-66 Appendix I version (basic), aircraft type with airframe/engine combination, course scope (theory/practical/both; complete/partial/difference; interface covered), start/end dates, location, type evaluation end date. Use to support Part-66 licence applications/type endorsements and check prerequisites.
- **Validation rules**: B2 basic approval implies B2L all system ratings; basic module subcontracting limited to 1-6, 8-10; type subcontracting limited to powerplant/avionics; exam privilege requires matching training privilege (except type exam where type training not required).
- **Training organisation (MTO) module, if targeted**: Form 11 approval schedule data model (class/category/limitation incl. TB1.1...TA.4, TL, T1-T4 with types); MTOE revision tracking (only revisions affecting certificate content trigger Form 11 reissue); change-notification workflow (pre-approval, Form 4 for nominated persons, new application for location/course additions).
- **Staff records**: instructor/examiner/assessor register with the AMC 147.A.110 fields, terms of reference, 24-month updating-training due date (35 h typical, can be split), schedule vs actual dates. Access control/audit trail (records must not be alterable without authorisation).
- **Student records**: unlimited retention (no auto-delete); attendance tracking with 90 % tuition / 95 % content thresholds before CoR issue; max 6 h theory/day; practical ratio >= 30 % in real maintenance environment; 15:1 practical ratio checks; course hour minima per Appendix I and conversion tables; theory % ranges.
- **Exam integrity**: incident log for cheating (12-month ban date computed, CA notification due within 1 calendar month); examiner disqualification; question bank security; controlled-environment evidence (ID verification, proctoring) for remote/URL exams.
- **Quality/audit module**: 12-month audit plan covering all Part-147 points (Form 22 Part 2 list is a ready-made checklist); small org contracted audits twice per year incl. one unannounced; subcontractor pre-audit and 12-month renewal audit; finding tracker with level, due date, closure date, reference doc; level 1 3-day clock; level 2 up to 6 months (3+3 escalation QM -> AM); annual AM review.
- **Distance learning / LMS integration**: traceable student activity logs, system requirements disclosure, third-party data security/integrity agreements.
- Library/document control: AMC lists document types an MTO library should hold - same document taxonomy (AD, SB, AMM, AMP, release docs) as the CAW app.

# PART-T — Continuing airworthiness of third-country-registered aircraft dry-leased-in by EU air carriers

## 1. Scope (point by point)

- **T.1 Competent authority**: the authority designated by the Member State that issued the operator's AOC oversees the aircraft and organisations.
  - GM T.1: Part-T applies (Art. 1(b), Art. 3(6) of 1321/2014 as amended by 2015/1536) to third-country-registered aircraft whose safety oversight has **not** been delegated to a Member State, when dry leased-in by an air carrier licensed under Reg. (EC) 1008/2008. If oversight has been delegated to a Member State, M.A.201 applies instead (Art. 1(a)(ii)). Dry lease-in approval conditions: ORO.AOC.110.

Section A
- **T.A.101 Scope**: ensures continuing airworthiness of Art. 1(b) aircraft meets Annex V essential requirements of 2018/1139; conditions for persons/organisations managing CAW and maintenance.
- **T.A.201 Responsibilities**: (1) operator responsible for airworthiness and must ensure: (a) type certificate issued or validated by EASA; (b) airworthy condition; (c) valid CofA per ICAO Annex 8; (d) maintenance per a maintenance programme complying with State of Registry requirements and ICAO Annex 6; (e) defects/damage affecting safe operation rectified to State of Registry standard; (f) compliance with State of Registry ADs/continued airworthiness requirements AND EASA mandatory safety information incl. ADs; (g) release to service after maintenance by qualified organisations per State of Registry, signed, with basic details of maintenance; (h) pre-flight inspection before each flight; (i) modifications and repairs comply with State of Registry requirements; (j) records kept (see below). (2) Tasks controlled by the operator's CAMO, which must meet Subpart G. (3) CAMO ensures maintenance and release by a Subpart E maintenance organisation, contracting one if it does not meet Subpart E itself.
- **T.A.501 Maintenance organisation** (Subpart E): CAMO ensures aircraft/components maintained by organisations that: (1) hold a maintenance organisation approval issued or accepted by the State of Registry; (2) scope covers the aircraft/component; (3) have an occurrence reporting system reporting unsafe conditions to the operator, the operator's CA, the TC/STC holder and the CAMO; (4) have an organisation manual describing all procedures.
- **T.A.701 Scope** (Subpart G): requirements additional to Part-CAMO for a CAMO to control T.A.201 tasks.
- **T.A.704 CAME**: in addition to CAMO.A.300, CAME contains procedures for Part-T compliance.
- **T.A.706 Personnel requirements**: in addition to CAMO.A.305, personnel under CAMO.A.305(a)(3)-(a)(5) and (b)(2) need adequate knowledge of the third-country laws of the State of Registry.
- **T.A.708 Continuing airworthiness management**: notwithstanding CAMO.A.315, CAMO must: (a) take aircraft to a maintenance organisation when needed; (b) ensure maintenance per the maintenance programme; (c) apply T.A.201(1)(f) mandatory information; (d) ensure defects corrected by the maintenance organisation per State-of-Registry-acceptable maintenance data; (e) coordinate scheduled maintenance, MSI, LLP replacement and component inspection; (f) manage and archive T.A.201(1)(j) records; (g) ensure mods/repairs approved per State of Registry.
- **T.A.709 Documentation**: notwithstanding CAMO.A.325, hold and use maintenance data acceptable to the State of Registry.
- **T.A.711 Privileges**: Part-CAMO organisation may perform T.A.708 tasks for aircraft on its AOC provided CA-approved procedures exist.
- **T.A.712 Management system**: in addition to CAMO.A.200, ensure compliance with Part-T.
- **T.A.714 Record-keeping**: in addition to CAMO.A.220(a), keep T.A.201(1)(j) records.
- **T.A.715 Continued validity**: in addition to CAMO.A.135, comply with Part-T and grant CA access to facilities, aircraft, documents incl. subcontracted activities.
- **T.A.716 Findings**: after T.B.705 notification: root cause and contributing factors; corrective action plan; demonstrate corrective action - within the CA-set period.

Section B
- **T.B.101 Scope**: CA administrative requirements.
- **T.B.102 Competent authority**: designated CA with documented procedures, structure, adequate staff, qualified/trained staff (initial and continuation), compliance procedures.
- **T.B.104 Record-keeping**: M.B.104(a),(b),(c) apply; per-aircraft minimum records; available on request to other Member State, EASA or State of Registry; retained 4 years after end of dry lease-in.
- **T.B.105 Mutual exchange of information**: M.B.105 applies.
- **T.B.201 Responsibilities**: CA conducts inspections, investigations, aircraft surveys; must inspect before approving the dry lease-in agreement (ARO.OPS.110(a)(1)) to verify T.A.201 compliance; coordinates with State of Registry.
- **T.B.202 Findings**: level 1 = significant non-compliance that lowers safety standard and seriously hazards flight safety; level 2 = non-compliance that could lower safety standard and possibly hazard flight safety; CA takes measures (e.g. grounding) and requires corrective action; level 1 requires corrective action before further flight and notification of the State of Registry.
- **T.B.702 Initial certification procedure**: in addition to CAMO.B.310, CA verifies Part-T procedures and compliance.
- **T.B.704 Continuing oversight**: in addition to CAMO.B.305, CA surveys a relevant sample of Part-T aircraft each oversight planning cycle.
- **T.B.705 Findings and corrective actions**: CA applies CAMO.B.350.

## 2. Obligations with exact numbers

- Aircraft records (T.A.201(1)(j)) available until superseded by equivalent information but **not less than 24 months**.
- Maintenance contract normally for the lease duration, which should **not exceed 7 months** (AMC3 T.A.201(3)); individual work orders acceptable for unscheduled line maintenance and component maintenance up to engines.
- CA per-aircraft oversight records retained **4 years after end of dry lease-in** (T.B.104(4)).
- Pre-flight inspection **before each flight** (T.A.201(1)(h)).
- Level 1 finding: corrective action **before further flight** + notify State of Registry (T.B.202(4)).
- Suspension/revocation of CAMO certificate invalidates the AOC (CAME statement).

## 4. Defined Terms

- **Part-T aircraft**: aircraft registered in a third country, safety oversight not delegated to a Member State, dry leased-in by an EU-licensed air carrier (Art. 1(b)).
- **State of Registry**: the third country; its requirements govern AMP, defect rectification standard, mods/repairs, release to service, maintenance organisation approval, maintenance data.
- **MSI** (mandatory safety information): from State of Registry and EASA, including ADs.
- **Level 1 / Level 2 finding** (T.B.202 definitions above).
- **ICA**: instructions for continued airworthiness (part of applicable maintenance data, AMC T.A.709).
