# Chunk 20: Part-CAMO Section B (Authority Requirements) and the Appendices to Annex Vc and to its AMC/GM

Source: EASA Easy Access Rules for Continuing Airworthiness (Reg. (EU) No 1321/2014), chunk 20 (1,165 lines, read in full).
Regulation sources cited in the chunk: Reg. (EU) 2019/1383 (original Part-CAMO), Reg. (EU) 2022/410 (Form 14 Issue 6 / M.A.201(ea) group CAMO changes), Reg. (EU) 2023/203 (Part-IS information security, **applicable from 22 February 2026**). AMC/GM: ED Decisions 2020/002/R, 2022/017/R, 2023/010/R (Part-IS AMC/GM), 2023/013/R.

**UK caveat (general):** UK retained Part-CAMO (UK Reg 1321/2014 as amended by UK SIs) mirrors the 2019/1383 text but the UK CAA is the sole competent authority, there is no "Agency" (EASA) in the loop, "Member State" cooperation provisions become largely irrelevant, and the forms are "CAA Form 14" / CAA equivalents of Form 13. The Part-IS (Reg. 2023/203) additions (CAMO.B.125(c), B.135A, B.200(e), B.205(c), B.300(g)-IS, B.330A) and the 2022/410 changes (M.A.201(ea) group contracting, Form 14 Issue 6 AOC-number wording) are EU acts adopted after 31 Dec 2020 and are likely **not** in UK retained law unless separately adopted by the UK (guess; verify against current CAA CAP/UK Reg text). The oversight cycle numbers (24/36/48 months), finding levels and 3-month Level 2 deadline are very likely identical in the UK (guess, high confidence).

## 1. Scope (point by point, appendix by appendix)

### Section B — Authority Requirements (obligations on the competent authority, CA)

| Point | Topic | Gist |
|---|---|---|
| CAMO.B.005 | Scope | Section B = administrative and management system requirements for the CA implementing/enforcing Section A. |
| CAMO.B.115 | Oversight documentation | CA must give its staff all legislation, standards, rules, technical publications and related documents. |
| CAMO.B.120 | Means of compliance | EASA develops AMC; alternative means of compliance (AltMoC) allowed; CA evaluates AltMoC proposed by organisations (per CAMO.A.120) and its own; notification duties. GM1: AltMoC approved for one CA/CAMO must be re-processed before another uses it. |
| CAMO.B.125 | Information to the Agency | (a) notify EASA of significant problems applying the rules; (b) pass safety-significant info from occurrence reports received under CAMO.A.160; (c) [from 22 Feb 2026] pass safety-significant info from information security reports under IS.I.OR.230. AMC1/GM1-3 detail. |
| CAMO.B.135 | Immediate reaction to a safety problem | CA collects/analyses/disseminates safety info (without prejudice to Reg 376/2014 and 2015/1018); EASA analyses and distributes recommendations; CA takes measures and notifies affected persons/orgs immediately, plus EASA and other MS where combined action needed. |
| CAMO.B.135A | Immediate reaction to info-security incident/vulnerability [from 22 Feb 2026] | Same pattern as B.135 for information security incidents/vulnerabilities with potential aviation safety impact; coordinate with national cyber authorities. AMC1: confidentiality, careful recipient selection. GM1: two-part report. |
| CAMO.B.200 | Management system (of the CA) | Documented policies/procedures, sufficient personnel with planning, qualified personnel, facilities, compliance monitoring incl. internal audit and safety risk management, person responsible for compliance monitoring; field managers; information exchange with other CAs; procedures available to EASA; (e) [from 22 Feb 2026] comply with Part-IS.AR. |
| CAMO.B.205 | Allocation of tasks | Tasks may be allocated only to qualified entities (QE) under Annex VI of 2018/1139; documented assessment system and agreement; internal audit/SRM covers QE tasks; (c) [from 2026] ISMS oversight (CAMO.A.200A) tasks may go to a QE or national cyber authority. |
| CAMO.B.210 | Changes in the management system | CA identifies changes affecting its capability, updates its system for rule changes, notifies EASA. |
| CAMO.B.220 | Record-keeping | CA records list, list of certificates issued, **5-year minimum** retention, availability to other CAs/EASA. |
| CAMO.B.300 | Oversight principles | Verify compliance before certificate issue, continued compliance, implementation of safety measures; based on assessments, audits and inspections incl. unannounced; multi-State facilities; info collection; (g) cooperation where M.A.201(ea) contract exists; second (g) [from 2026] review of IS.I.OR.200(e)/IS.D.OR.200(e) approvals. |
| CAMO.B.305 | Oversight programme | Risk-based programme; cycle **≤24 months**, extendable to **36** and **48**; may be reduced; records of due/done dates; recommendation report at end of each cycle. |
| CAMO.B.310 | Initial certification procedure | Verify compliance; meet accountable manager at least once; record findings; all findings closed before issue; issue EASA Form 14; formally approve CAME; unlimited duration; approve procedure for changes not requiring prior approval. |
| CAMO.B.330 | Changes | Changes requiring prior approval: verify, set conditions during change, approve; unapproved implementation = suspend/limit/revoke; changes not requiring prior approval: assess notification. |

| CAMO.B.350 | Findings and corrective actions | Level 1 / Level 2 definitions, mandatory Level 1 cases, actions, **3-month** initial Level 2 period, escalation. |
| CAMO.B.355 | Suspension, limitation and revocation | Grounds incl. **24-month** inability to do on-site audits due to security situation. |

## 2. Obligations (with exact numbers)

### 2.1 Means of compliance (CAMO.B.120)
- CA must have a system to consistently evaluate all AltMoC used by itself or organisations it oversees.
- For organisation-proposed AltMoC (CAMO.A.120): analyse documentation, inspect if necessary; if compliant, "without undue delay": (1) notify applicant it may implement, amend approval/certificate if applicable; (2) notify EASA of content with copies of documentation.
- For CA's own AltMoC: make available to all orgs/persons under oversight; notify EASA without undue delay with full description, procedure revisions and compliance assessment.
- GM1: an AltMoC accepted elsewhere must be **processed again** under B.120(d)/(e) before another CA/CAMO uses it (no automatic portability).

### 2.2 Information to EASA (CAMO.B.125)
- AMC1: each CA appoints a **coordinator** as contact point for safety-significant information exchange.
- GM1 definition of "safety-significant information stemming from occurrence reports" — see Defined Terms.
- GM2: conclusive safety analysis content: (a) detailed description incl. scenario; (b) stakeholders affected (operation/organisation types); as appropriate (c) risk assessment of severity and probability of all consequences; (d) existing safety barriers; (e) mitigating actions in place/developed; (f) recommendations; (g) any other essential element.
- GM3: occurrences concerning EASA-certified orgs/products to be notified to EASA if: reportable under applicable regulation; responsible org is EASA-certified; and the MS CA concludes the org has not been informed, or the occurrence was not properly addressed/left unattended. Format compatible with **ECCAIRS**, with attachments.

### 2.3 Immediate reaction (CAMO.B.135 / B.135A)
- Measures taken by the CA must be **immediately** notified to everyone who must comply, plus EASA, plus other MS when combined action required.
- B.135A (info security, from 22 Feb 2026): same structure; coordination with national info-security/cyber authorities. AMC1: confidentiality of collection; select recipients so reports cannot be exploited (e.g. revealing uncorrected vulnerabilities). GM1: optional two-step mechanism — an alert with mostly public info, then sensitive data via secure, need-to-know channels; use agreed taxonomy where possible.

### 2.4 CA management system (CAMO.B.200 + AMC/GM)
- Minimum elements (a)(1)–(6): documented, current procedures; sufficient personnel with availability planning; qualified personnel with initial and recurrent training; adequate facilities/offices; compliance monitoring with internal audit and safety risk management and feedback of audit findings to senior management; person(s) ultimately responsible for compliance monitoring.
- (b) one or more persons with overall responsibility per field of activity (including management system).
- (c) procedures for mutual exchange of information/assistance with other CAs incl. findings and follow-up on organisations certified elsewhere but active in the territory.
- (d) procedures and amendments available to EASA for standardisation, and to regulated orgs on request.
- (e) from 22 Feb 2026: comply with Part-IS.AR.
- AMC1 organisational structure: consider number of certificates, size of CAMOs, use of QEs/other MS resources, activity level, growth; retain effective control (CAMOs must not "in effect, regulate themselves"); no reliance on single individuals (cover illness/leave).
- AMC2: general policy set by highest-level manager; known by all staff; take into account 2018/1139, IRs/AMC/CS/GM, industry needs, EASA/CA needs; define objectives, control procedures and measurement.
- AMC1 (a)(1): documented procedures cover minimum (1) policy and objectives; (2) organisational structure; (3) responsibilities and authority; (4) procedures and processes; (5) internal/external interfaces; (6) internal control procedures; (7) training of personnel; (8) cross-references; (9) assistance from other CAs/EASA. Index of cross-references recommended.
- GM1 (a)(2) Sufficient personnel (staffing model):
  - Quantitative: estimated initial certificates; number of orgs certified; estimated number of subcontracted orgs used.
  - Qualitative: size/nature/complexity (privileges, approval types/scope, industry-standard certification, personnel numbers, structure/subsidiaries); safety priorities; past oversight results (number and level of findings, corrective action time frames, management system maturity); industry size/growth.
  - Estimate standard working time for new certificate applications, number of new certificates and changes per planning period.
  - Planning data: standard number of audits per cycle; standard audit duration; standard working time for preparation, on-site, reporting, follow-up per inspector; standard number of unannounced inspections; standard inspection duration; minimum number and qualification of inspectors per audit/inspection.
  - Units: hours or working days per inspector, consistently.
  - Recommends a **spreadsheet application** to compute total hours/days per oversight planning cycle; basis for personnel availability planning.
  - Available inspector time net of admin tasks, training, other projects, planned absence, reserve for unplanned tasks; consider QEs, multi-MS cooperation, bilateral agreements.
  - Outcome: monitor audit/inspection due and done dates; plan personnel; identify gaps.
- AMC1 (a)(3) training general: per inspector define competencies, minimum qualifications, initial/recurrent programmes, ensure training meets standards and is reviewed. Trainers may be internal or external; non-internal trainers' skills assessed; records of training and assessment.
- AMC2 (a)(3) inspector qualifications:
  - practical experience in aviation safety standards;
  - comprehensive knowledge of relevant IRs/CS/GM, CA procedures, inspector rights/obligations, SMS (EU management system incl. compliance monitoring, ICAO Annex 19), continuing airworthiness management incl. maintenance programme development/control, operational procedures affecting CAW, maintenance HF;
  - training in auditing and evaluating management systems and SRM;
  - **5 years** relevant work experience to work independently (may include training time for (a)(5));
  - relevant engineering degree (aeronautical, mechanical, electrical, electronic, avionic or other relevant) or aircraft maintenance technician qualification with additional education;
  - type knowledge of a "relevant sample" of aircraft via formal course at least equivalent to **Part-66 Appendix III Level 1 General Familiarisation**;
  - knowledge of maintenance standards incl. **FTS training** (Appendix III to AMC4 CAMO.A.305(g)).
  - Integrity, impartiality, tact. Undesirable for inspectors to obtain technical qualifications from entities they directly oversee.
- AMC3 (a)(3) initial training (12 topics): aviation legislation/organisation; Chicago Convention/ICAO Annexes/Docs; Reg 376/2014; overview of 2018/1139 and AMC/CS/GM; Reg 1321/2014; management systems incl. effectiveness assessment, hazard ID, risk assessment, just culture; auditing techniques; CA procedures; HF; inspector rights/obligations; on-the-job training (OJT); technical/aircraft-specific training. CA assesses competency before unsupervised work. Recurrent: legislation/environment/technology changes; CA procedures; technical; results of past oversight. **Competency assessment at intervals not exceeding 3 years**, recorded with resulting actions.
- AMC1 (a)(5) CA safety risk management: documented hazard ID means and data sources (incl. other CAs); analysis (probability/severity), assessment (tolerability), control (mitigation); responsibilities for hazard ID, follow-up; management levels deciding tolerability; effectiveness assessment; link to compliance monitoring. Evidence: trained staff; documented hazards/risk assessments; regular management meetings; triggered at minimum on changes affecting capability; **record of mitigation actions with status and owner**; follow-up; effectiveness assessment; periodic review of assessments.
- GM1 (a)(5): SRM addresses risks to the CA's own capability; not a substitute for State SRM (ICAO Annex 19 Ch 3, 3.3) but can use SSP data; applies to management of change (B.210).
- AMC1 B.200(d): procedures provided to EASA must include organisational structure for oversight, minimum qualification/experience of oversight staff and appointment principles, how assessments/certificates/oversight/findings/enforcement/safety concerns are done, exemptions/derogations principles, safety information distribution, criteria for planning oversight incl. interfaces (air ops vs CAW), outline initial and recurrent training. EASA may request working-method details. Electronic submission allowed.

### 2.5 Allocation of tasks to qualified entities (CAMO.B.205)
- Only to qualified entities. CA must have: documented system to initially and continuously assess QE compliance with Annex VI of 2018/1139; documented agreement approved by both parties at appropriate management level defining (i) tasks, (ii) declarations/reports/records to provide, (iii) technical conditions, (iv) liability coverage, (v) information protection.
- Internal audit and SRM must cover tasks done on its behalf.
- (c) [from 2026] ISMS oversight (CAMO.A.200A) tasks: to QE or national cyber authority; ensure aviation safety coordination, integration of results into the organisation's oversight files, CA's own ISMS covers them.
- GM1: tasks may cover initial certification and continuing oversight under Reg 1321/2014.

### 2.6 CA changes (CAMO.B.210)
- System to identify capability-affecting changes; timely update for rule changes; notify EASA.

### 2.7 CA record-keeping (CAMO.B.220)
- Records to store with accessibility and traceability: (1) management system policies/procedures; (2) personnel training, qualification, authorisation; (3) task allocation (B.205 elements and task details); (4) certification and oversight of each org: (i) application; (ii) oversight programme incl. all assessment/audit/inspection records; (iii) certificate incl. changes; (iv) copy of oversight programme with audit due and done dates; (v) formal correspondence; (vi) findings, corrective actions, **date of action closure**, exemptions, enforcement; (vii) reports from other CAs under B.300(d); (viii) all CAMEs/manuals and amendments; (ix) any other approved document; (5) AltMoC evaluation/notification; (6) safety information and follow-up (B.125); (7) use of flexibility provisions.
- (b) list of all organisation certificates issued.
- (c) **minimum 5 years**, subject to data protection law.
- (d) available on request to another MS CA or EASA.
- AMC1 (a): accessible within reasonable time; traceable/retrievable throughout retention; sensitive data stored securely with controlled access; paper, electronic, microfilm, optical disc acceptable; legible; **retention starts when the record is created**; computer systems need **at least one backup updated within 24 hours of any new entry**; safeguards against unauthorised alteration; backup hardware **in a different location** from working data; data migration on hardware/software change must preserve access for full retention period.
- AMC1 (a)(1): management system records include policies/procedures, personnel files with training/qualification support documents, internal audit and SRM results (findings, corrective/preventive/mitigation actions), QE contracts.
- AMC1 (d): record requests from other MS/EASA limited to: incidents/accidents; ACAM findings involving orgs approved by another CA (root cause); aircraft mainly operated in another MS; aircraft previously operated in another MS; orgs with approvals in several MS. Reason must be stated; copy or consultation.

### 2.8 Oversight principles (CAMO.B.300)
- Verify: pre-issue compliance, continued compliance, implementation of CA-mandated safety measures (B.135(c),(d)).
- Verification: supported by guidance documentation for oversight staff; results provided to the organisation; based on assessments, audits and inspections **including unannounced inspections**; provides evidence for enforcement (B.350).
- Scope considers past oversight results and safety priorities.
- (d) Facilities in more than one State: CA (per CAMO.A.105) may agree to have oversight done by the CA where facilities are, or EASA for third-country facilities; organisation must be informed of agreement and scope.
- (e) Inform the other State's CA (or EASA for third-country principal place of business) **before** on-site audit/inspection at foreign facilities.
- (f) Collect and process useful information incl. for unannounced inspections. AMC1 (f) minimum: occurrence reports received; results indicating a Part-CAMO-originated issue from ramp inspections (Part-ARO Subpart RAMP, Reg 965/2012), product surveys (M.B.303 / ML.B.303), aircraft sample surveys (B.305(b)(1)), physical surveys or partial airworthiness reviews by the CA (M.B.901).
- (g) Where a contract under M.A.201(ea) (CAMO contracted by an air carrier operator within a group; from Reg 2022/410) exists: CAMO's CA and operator(s)' CAs cooperate and exchange oversight results; operator CA may perform oversight tasks on the CAMO.
- second (g) [from 22 Feb 2026]: for CAMO.A.200A, review any approval granted under IS.I.OR.200(e) or IS.D.OR.200(e) (Del. Reg 2022/1645) following the applicable oversight audit cycle and whenever scope of work changes. (Note: text contains two points labelled (g).)
- AMC1 (a)(b)(c): initially assess that management system enablers are present and suitable; in continuing oversight verify they remain present and operational and assess effectiveness; re-review assessment on significant changes.
- GM1 (a)(b)(c) maturity levels: present / suitable / operating / effective (see Defined Terms).
- GM1 (g) cooperative oversight agreement contents:
  - (a) General: scope and identification of operator(s) and CAMO (one agreement may cover several operators using the same CAMO); focal points (changing them does not need full revision); meetings; conflict resolution.
  - (b) Oversight: share oversight programmes (audit plan/programme, complexity assessment); share management system assessment approach and results; oversight tasks on CAMO by operator CA on behalf of CAMO CA; observers or joint audits; support when aircraft moves between AOCs within group; share info on how CAMO/operator manage tasks — product audit aircraft selection informed by operator CA; tech log and MEL usage info to operator CA; interfaces on non-mandatory mods/inspections decisions (CAMO.A.315(b)(4)); pre-flight inspection system.
  - (c) Additional: methods/timing of sharing (e.g. management system assessment results shared each time); prompt reaction to serious concerns; info before integrating an aircraft into the AOC; info before granting specific approvals (e.g. Part-SPA, AMC 20-6); sharing occurrence reports and coordinated follow-up procedures; sharing exposition/manual changes affecting harmonised procedures.
- GM2 (g) harmonised management systems: common assessment approach (not necessarily same tool); finding on a harmonised procedure or group standard → inform other CAs, share root cause analysis and corrective action plan; other CAs decide action (none, communicate, or raise own finding). Cycle durations may differ across group organisations (e.g. 24 or less vs 36 or 48 months); CAs inform each other of increases/reductions.

### 2.9 Oversight programme and planning cycle (CAMO.B.305) — key numbers
- Programme based on nature, complexity, past results, risk. Each cycle includes: assessments, audits, inspections incl. unannounced, and as applicable (i) management system assessments and process audits; (ii) **product audits of a relevant sample of aircraft** managed; (iii) **sampling of airworthiness reviews** performed; (iv) **sampling of permits to fly** issued; plus (2) **meetings between accountable manager and CA**.
- (c) Oversight planning cycle **not exceeding 24 months**.
- (d) Extension **up to 36 months** if during the **previous 24 months**: (1) effective hazard identification and risk management demonstrated; (2) continuously demonstrated full control over all changes (CAMO.A.130); (3) **no Level 1 findings**; (4) all corrective actions implemented within accepted/extended time (B.350).
- Further extension to **maximum 48 months** if additionally the organisation has established, and CA has approved, an **effective continuous reporting system** to the CA on safety performance and regulatory compliance.
- (e) Cycle may be **reduced** if evidence of decreased safety performance.
- (f) Records of due and actual dates for audits, inspections and meetings.
- (g) At completion of each cycle: **recommendation report on continuation of approval** (in practice, new EASA Form 13-CAMO — AMC1 B.305(b)(1)(d)).
- AMC1 (a);(b): **annual review** of the cycle and programme per organisation; consider CAMO.A.160 info and B.300(f) info.
- AMC1 (b): programme/product audits consider: management system effectiveness; industry standards implemented; scope/procedure of changes not requiring prior approval; AltMoC-specific procedures; number of approved locations and activities at each; number and type of subcontractors doing CAW management tasks; volume of activity per aircraft type/series/group.
- AMC2 (b): **all subcontracted organisations audited by CA at periods not exceeding the applicable cycle** (credits per AMC2 B.305(c)(d) allowed); inspector accompanied by a senior technical member of the CAMO; CA ensures CAMO has sufficient control over the subcontractor (AMC1 CAMO.A.125(d)(3)).
- AMC1 (b)(1): programme indicates which aspects each audit covers; part of each audit reviews the org's compliance monitoring audit reports; audit report lists areas/processes audited and all findings; **new EASA Form 13-CAMO issued at the completion of each oversight planning cycle**.
- AMC1 (c): risk-based schedule taking into account the organisation's own hazard identification and risk assessment; if frequency varied, all aspects still covered within cycle.
- AMC2 (c): all processes completely audited within the cycle; **first cycle normally starts at date of issue of first certificate**; to align with calendar year, shorten the first cycle; interval between two audits of the same process ≤ cycle; **at least one on-site audit per cycle**; multi-site selection based on past results, volume, risk; integrated oversight for orgs with multiple certificates, with credit for identical audit items if (1) same item for all certificates, (2) evidence of completion and corrective actions closed, (3) no evidence of deterioration, (4) interval for that item ≤ cycle.
- AMC1 (d) beyond 24 months: **at least one focused inspection within each 12-month segment** of the cycle; credit possible for linked inspections (e.g. ACAM survey of a managed aircraft); if results show decreased performance/compliance → **revert to 24-month (or less)** cycle and revise programme; to approve **beyond 36 months**, CA agrees format and contents of the organisation's continuous reporting.

### 2.10 Initial certification (CAMO.B.310)
- (a) verify compliance on application.
- (b) meeting with accountable manager **at least once** during the investigation, so he/she understands significance of certification and the reason for signing the CAME compliance statement.
- (c) record all findings, closure actions and recommendations.
- (d) confirm findings in writing; **all findings corrected before certificate issued**. AMC1 (d): written confirmation **within 2 weeks of the on-site audit**.
- (e) issue EASA Form 14 (Appendix I) and formally approve CAME. AMC1 (e)(2): approval of CAME in writing.
- (f) certificate reference number per EASA format.
- (g) **unlimited duration**; privileges, scope and limitations in terms of approval.
- (h) approve the CAME procedure for changes not requiring prior approval (CAMO.A.130(c)) — scope, management and notification of such changes.
- AMC1 B.310: verification audit incl. interviews and facility inspections; only conducted once application, supporting docs and the **organisation's pre-audit** results are satisfactory. Focus areas: management structure incl. names/qualifications of CAMO.A.305(a) and (b)(2) personnel; staff numbers/qualifications/experience vs. intended terms; validity of licences/authorisations; SRM and compliance monitoring; facilities; documentation incl. M.A.201(ea) CAW management contract, CAME procedure compliance, accountable manager signature of exposition statement. If refused, inform applicant of right of appeal under national law.
- GM1 B.310/B.330: CA may request translation of documents submitted in another language (M.A.201(ea) context).
- AMC1 (a) audit organisation: team vs. series of audits; may be structured by product line (e.g. full check on A320, sample on A310 for weak areas); consider privileges (e.g. airworthiness reviews); inspector accompanied by senior member, normally the compliance monitoring manager; inform of all findings at end.
- AMC1 (c): unsure items flagged to the organisation and reviewed internally (verbal confirmation if no finding); findings recorded on audit report form with **provisional** Level 1/2 categorisation, later reviewed and changed to **confirmed**.
- AMC2 (c): record audit on **EASA Form 13-CAMO**; reviewed by a competent independent person nominated by the CA (relevant Part-CAMO points, finding levels, closure actions), signature on Form 13-CAMO; reports include **date each finding was closed** with reference to CA report/letter confirming closure.
- AMC1 (e)(2): subcontracting contracts included in CAME; CA verifies AMC1 CAMO.A.125(d)(3) standards; consider subcontractor's other contracts regardless of State of registry (resources, expertise, management, facilities, liaison incl. contracted maintenance orgs); **CAME approval = formal acceptance of personnel in CAMO.A.305(a), (b)(2), (e) and (f)**; CA may reject an accountable manager with clear evidence of previous abuse of a senior position in an approved organisation; for CAT, commercial SPO, commercial ATO/DTO, initial approval of the aircraft technical log system (M.A.306(b), M.B.305) may be by approving the CAME describing it. (Text contains two points labelled (e).)

### 2.11 Changes (CAMO.B.330)
- Prior-approval changes: verify compliance; set conditions to operate during change (unless suspension needed); approve.
- Implementing prior-approval changes without approval → CA **shall suspend, limit or revoke** the certificate (without prejudice to other enforcement).
- Non-prior-approval changes: assess the CAMO.A.130(c) notification; on non-compliance notify and request further changes; for Level 1/2 findings act per B.350.
- AMC1: adequate control of changes to CAMO.A.305(a), (b)(2), (e), (f) personnel (requires CAME amendment); for new nominees for (a), (b)(2), (e) CA may require written résumé, interview or more evidence; prior-approval changes → audit limited to extent of change, decide whether a risk assessment is needed, inform organisation, request and assess it if needed; interviews/inspections if required; use applicable parts of Form 13-CAMO.
- GM1 name change: on application plus CAME amendment, **reissue certificate**; no audit unless evidence of other changes.
- GM1 B.310(e)(1)/B.330 (terms of approval): changes to detailed CAME scope of work within limits already on Form 14 (directly or via capability list) may be treated as not affecting the approval and handled via the CAMO.A.130(c) non-prior-approval procedure.

### 2.12 ISMS changes (CAMO.B.330A, from 22 Feb 2026)
- IS.I.OR.255(a) notified changes reviewed in continuing oversight per B.300; non-compliance → notify, request changes, act per B.350.
- IS.I.OR.255(b) changes requiring approval: check compliance, set conditions during implementation, approve when satisfied.

### 2.13 Findings (CAMO.B.350)
- System to analyse findings for safety significance.
- **Level 1**: significant non-compliance with requirements, procedures/manuals, or terms of approval/certificate "which lowers safety or seriously endangers flight safety". Mandatory Level 1 cases:
  1. failure to give CA access to facilities (CAMO.A.140) during normal operating hours **after two written requests**;
  2. obtaining/maintaining certificate validity by **falsification** of documentary evidence;
  3. evidence of **malpractice or fraudulent use** of the certificate;
  4. **lack of an accountable manager**.
- **Level 2**: any non-compliance "which may lower safety or endanger flight safety".
- Communicate in writing and request corrective action. If finding directly relates to an aircraft, inform the **State of registry**.
- Level 1: immediate action to prohibit or limit activities; if appropriate revoke, limit or suspend wholly/partly until successful corrective action.
- Level 2: corrective action implementation period appropriate to nature, **initially not more than 3 months**, starting **from the date of written communication** of the finding; may be extended at end of period subject to nature, past safety performance and a satisfactory corrective action plan agreed by CA; CA assesses and accepts corrective action and implementation plan.
- Failure to submit acceptable plan or to implement within accepted/extended time → **escalated to Level 1**.
- Record all findings raised or received under (e), enforcement measures, corrective actions and **date of action closure**.
- (e) A MS CA overseeing under B.300(d) that finds non-compliance by an org certified by another CA/EASA informs that CA with an indication of the finding level.
- Note: Section B here has no "observation"/Level 3 category (Level 3 / observations are not in this text).

### 2.14 Suspension, limitation, revocation (CAMO.B.355)
- (a) suspend on reasonable grounds for potential safety threat;
- (b) suspend, revoke or limit pursuant to B.350;
- (c) suspend if inspectors unable **over a period of 24 months** to do on-site audits due to the security situation where facilities are located.
- AMC1 (c): EC Security Directorate advises against non-essential travel to countries with: armed conflict with frequent confrontation/casualties/infrastructure damage; situation that could lead to war or high tension, very poorly functioning institutions; relatively frequent terrorist attacks with authorities unable to ensure security; frequent criminal violence targeting non-nationals. Such countries are not compatible with on-site audits.

## 4. Defined Terms

- **Safety-significant information stemming from occurrence reports** (GM1 CAMO.B.125(b)): "(a) a conclusive safety analysis which summarises individual occurrence data and provides an in-depth analysis of a safety issue, and which may be relevant for the Agency's safety action planning; and (b) individual occurrence data for the cases where the Agency is the competent authority and which fulfils the reporting criteria of GM3 CAMO.B.125(b)."
- **Management system maturity levels** (GM1 CAMO.B.300(a);(b);(c)):
  - '**present**': evidence the process/feature is documented in the management system/SMS documentation;
  - '**suitable**': suitable for size, nature, complexity and inherent risk;
  - '**operating**': evidence it is in use and producing an output;
  - '**effective**': evidence it achieves the desired outcome and has a positive safety impact.
- **Level 1 finding**: significant non-compliance "which lowers safety or seriously endangers flight safety" (plus the four mandatory cases).
- **Level 2 finding**: non-compliance "which may lower safety or endanger flight safety".
- **Closure actions**: "actions required to close a finding" (CAMO.B.310(c)).
- **Oversight planning cycle**: period within which all oversight activities/processes must be covered; ≤24, ≤36 or ≤48 months.
- **Focused inspection**: "inspection of a specific area, element or aspect of the organisation" (AMC1 CAMO.B.305(d)).
- **Relevant engineering degree**: engineering degree from aeronautical, mechanical, electrical, electronic, avionic or other studies relevant to maintenance and continuing airworthiness of aircraft/components (AMC2 CAMO.B.200(a)(3)).
- **Relevant sample** (of aircraft types for inspector training): courses covering typical aircraft and systems within the scope of work.
- **Qualified entity**: entity meeting Annex VI 'Essential requirements for qualified entities' of Reg 2018/1139 to which certification/oversight tasks may be allocated.
- **Alternative means of compliance (AltMoC)**: means other than EASA AMC used to establish compliance; subject to CA evaluation and EASA notification.
- **Cooperative oversight agreement**: agreement between CAMO's CA and operator CA(s) under M.A.201(ea) contracting (GM1 CAMO.B.300(g)).
- **Harmonised management systems / group standard**: management systems harmonised across an air carrier business grouping per M.A.201(ea).
- Abbreviations used: ACAM (aircraft continuing airworthiness monitoring), ECCAIRS, SSP (State Safety Programme), SARPs, OJT, HF, FTS, ATL, MEL, CDL, AOG, MCF, ERP, AMP, ARC, LLP, IPC, AMM, CRS, MOE, AOC, QE, SPA, SPO, ATO, DTO, MTOM, ELA1.

## 5. Software Implications (Airworthiness+)

Most of Section B binds the authority, but it defines what the authority will ask for and how it grades the CAMO — directly useful for CAMO-facing features (audit readiness, finding tracking, CAME management).

1. **Approval certificate model (Form 14)**: store `reference` (pattern `{STATE}.CAMO.{NNNN}`, e.g. UK.CAMO.xxxx), linked AOC number(s), organisation name/address, competent authority, date of original issue, revision date, revision no, CAME reference and CAME section, and a terms-of-approval table rows: aircraft type/series/group (free text with typed options: TC type designation, Part-66 type rating/series, group e.g. Group 3/ELA1 categories/MTOM band, optional engine) + boolean flags airworthiness review authorised, permits to fly authorised, subcontracted organisations. Status: active / suspended / limited / revoked / surrendered / superseded. Unlimited duration — no expiry date field; validity driven by status. For AOC-variant certificates, model the dependency: AOC suspension/revocation auto-invalidates the CAMO certificate for those registrations (unless M.A.201(ea) contracted CAMO).
2. **Scope check engine**: enforce that managed aircraft fall within both Form 14 terms and the narrower CAME scope of work/capability list; warn when adding an aircraft type outside Form 14 (requires prior-approval change, CAMO.A.130(a)) vs. within Form 14 but outside CAME (can follow non-prior-approval procedure, CAMO.A.130(c)). Gate ARC issue and permit-to-fly issue on the Form 14 flags.
3. **Findings tracker** (CAMO side receiving CA findings, and reused for internal compliance monitoring): fields — finding ID, source (CA audit, other CA via B.350(e), internal), audit reference, Form 13 Part 2/3 cross-reference (CAMO.A.xxx or CAME section), level (1/2; provisional vs confirmed), description, date written communication received (clock start), due date (default = +3 months for Level 2), extension(s) with justification and agreed CAP, corrective action plan acceptance date, root cause, closure date, closure reference (CA letter/report). Alerts: approaching Level 2 deadline; automatic warning that missing the deadline escalates to Level 1. Level 1 flags should trigger "activity prohibited/limited" banners. If a finding relates to a specific aircraft, link it (CA informs State of registry).
4. **Oversight cycle planner**: track CA oversight planning cycle (start = date of first certificate unless shortened to calendar alignment), length (24/36/48), annual review date, focused inspection per 12-month segment when >24, AM–CA meetings, product audit sample aircraft, sampled ARCs and permits to fly, subcontractor audits. Dashboard showing extension eligibility criteria over the last 24 months: no Level 1 findings, all corrective actions on time, change control demonstrated, hazard/risk management effective; and for 48 months, an approved continuous reporting system — offer a "continuous safety performance and compliance report" export whose format the CA agrees.
5. **CAME authoring/compliance module**: use the Form 13-CAMO Part 3 structure (Parts 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 4B, 5 with sub-items listed above) as the default CAME template and as a self-assessment checklist with tick/X/N/A/N/R states; similarly the Part 2 checklist of CAMO.A.115–A.325 (add CAMO.A.200A ISMS item for EU from 22 Feb 2026). Version control with CAME revision number/date, flag whether a revision affects Form 14 content (needs certificate reissue) or not.
6. **Change management workflow**: classify change as prior-approval vs not; capture conditions set by CA during change; block implementing prior-approval changes before approval (enforcement: suspend/limit/revoke). Nominated persons changes (CAMO.A.305(a), (b)(2), (e), (f)) → CAME amendment, résumé upload, interview status. Name change → certificate reissue, no audit by default.
7. **Pre-audit/initial certification pack**: generate application package (Form 2 elsewhere), pre-audit results, nominated-person qualifications and licence validity, facility info, M.A.201(ea) contract, signed AM exposition statement; track findings that must all be closed before issue; record 2-week written-confirmation expectation.
8. **Subcontractor and maintenance contract management**: structured contract records built from Appendix II and IV checklists (scope incl. registrations, data provision, AD/SB flows, FH/FC feed frequency, LLP status, defect deferral authority, records ownership, meeting schedule, CA access clause, monitoring rights). Pre-subcontract audit record; nominated person for subcontractor procedure acceptance; CAME Part 5 lists (5.2–5.7) auto-generated from these records. Provide CAMO-owned records with subcontractor online access (explicitly acceptable).
9. **Data exchange**: APIs/exports for hours/cycles, AD status (due date + selected means of compliance), LLP/time-controlled component status, delivery documentation (CRS, mods, repairs, ADs accomplished, visit report, bench test). Fast-track urgent maintenance data channel.
10. **Record retention and integrity** (mirroring CA AMC, also good practice): retention clocks starting at record creation; minimum 5 years for CA-side; backups updated within 24 h, stored at a different location; tamper protection/audit trail; migration preserves access; access control for sensitive data.
11. **Occurrence and info-security reporting**: ECCAIRS-compatible export (EU) for occurrences; for Part-IS (EU, from 22 Feb 2026) support two-part reports (public alert + restricted sensitive part) and need-to-know distribution. UK: likely MOR via CAA/ECCAIRS-compatible portal; Part-IS likely not applicable (guess).
12. **Group/M.A.201(ea) support (EU only, likely not UK)**: multi-operator links per CAMO, harmonised procedures flag, propagate findings on harmonised procedures to related organisations.
13. **Inspector/staff competency** (optional authority-facing or internal auditor module): qualifications, 5-year experience, Part-66 App III Level 1 type courses, FTS training, competency assessment ≤3 years.
14. **Security-situation suspension**: record if a site has been inaccessible for on-site audit; 24-month trigger for suspension (relevant to CAMOs with remote facilities).
