AMC to 1(b), 3.2 and 4.2 (practical element):
- Part of practical training should be in a real maintenance or manufacturer environment; classroom/simulators allowed for part.
- Tasks selected for frequency, complexity, variety, safety, criticality, novelty; should cover all chapters of the 3.2 table.
- "for aeroplanes with a MTOM equal or above 30000kg, the duration for the practical element of a type rating training course should not be less than two weeks" unless a shorter duration is justified to the competent authority.
- Training provider gives trainees a schedule/plan of tasks; completed tasks entered in a logbook which each task or group of tasks may be countersigned by the designated assessor.
- "Designated assessors appropriately qualified" = demonstrate training and experience in the assessment process and be authorised by the organisation (further guidance in Appendix III to AMC to Part-66).
- The practical element (for powerplant and avionic systems) may be subcontracted by a Part-147 organisation under 147.A.145(d)3.

### 2.2 Appendix III, 4. Type training examination and assessment standard

4.1 Theoretical element examination standard (2023/989):
- Multi-choice, "Each multi-choice question shall have 3 alternative answers of which only one shall be the correct answer." Time: "nominal average of 90 seconds per question".
- Distractors equally plausible; numerical distractors reflect procedural errors (wrong sense, wrong units), not random numbers.
- Level per chapter as in point 2 "Aircraft type training levels"; a limited number of lower-level questions acceptable.
- Closed book; exception for examining B1/B2 ability to interpret technical documents.
- "The number of questions shall be at least one question per hour of training." Questions per chapter/level proportionate to effective training hours (or anticipated average time for student-centred methods) and TNA learning objectives. Competent authority assesses number and level when approving the course.
- "The minimum examination pass mark is 75 %." If split into several exams, each passed with at least 75 %. "the number of questions in the examination shall be a multiple of 4."
- No penalty marking.
- End-of-module phase exams only count if they contain the correct number and level of questions.
- MBT learning programme questions must not be used in course or phase examinations.
- AMC1 4.1: examinations may be computer or paper based, or both (refer to 147.A.135).

4.2 Practical element assessment standard (1321/2014): performed by "designated assessors appropriately qualified"; evaluates knowledge and skills of the trainee.

### 2.3 Appendix III, 5. Type evaluation standard for Group 2 and Group 3 aircraft (2023/989)

- Conducted by Part-147 approved training organisations or the competent authority.
- Consists of practical assessment and oral examination. Oral exam on a sample of chapters from point 3 at the level in point 3.1(e).
- Objectives: discuss type and systems with confidence; safe performance of maintenance per manual (troubleshooting, repairs, adjustments, replacements, rigging, functional checks such as engine run); correct use of technical literature; correct use of special tooling/test equipment and removal/replacement of type-unique components.
- "The maximum number of attempts for each examination is three in a 12-month period. A waiting period of 30 days is required after the first failed attempt within one set, and a waiting period of 60 days is required after the second failed attempt."
- Applicant confirms in writing the number and dates of attempts in the last 12 months and where they took place; the MTO/authority checks.
- "The type evaluation shall be passed, and the required practical experience shall be completed within the 3 years preceding the application for the rating endorsement".
- At least one examiner present; examiner(s) must not have been involved in the applicant's training.
- Written and signed report explaining pass or fail, made available to the candidate.
- AMC to Section 5: this standard applies only where type examination substitutes for type training, not to exams within type training.

### 2.4 Appendix III, 6. On-the-job training (OJT) (2023/989)

6.1 General:
- (a) OJT task list and programme accepted by the competent authority that issued the licence before starting.
- (b) At one or more maintenance organisations approved for maintenance of that type; one controls the OJT. AMC: Part-145 or Part-CAO AMOs holding an "A" rating for the aircraft; the organisation conducting the final assessment controls and coordinates the whole programme; OJT procedures go in the exposition.
- (c) Applicant holds category A, B or L5 licence, or has finished theoretical type training and "cumulated at least 50 % of the basic experience requirement (point 66.A.30)".
- (d) "The applicant shall start and complete the OJT within 3 years preceding the application for the first type rating endorsement. At least 50 % of the OJT tasks shall take place after the related aircraft theoretical type training has been completed."
- (e) One-to-one mentorship by qualified mentor(s).
- (f) Each task signed off by the applicant, referencing an actual job card/work sheet; mentors verify and countersign.
- (g) On satisfactory completion mentors issue a recommendation for final assessment by designated assessors.

6.2 OJT logbook contents: see section 3.

6.3 Final assessment:
- Only once logbook completed and mentors' recommendation signed.
- Assessor(s) notify the licensing authority of the date "well in advance" to allow possible participation.
- "The final assessment shall have a minimum duration of one working day."
- Samples: general technical knowledge for the category; type-specific knowledge and skills; understanding of licence privileges; behaviour and safety attitude.
- "A failed assessment may be retaken after 3 months or, if additional training has been received and a new recommendation by the mentors has been made, earlier than 3 months if agreed by the assessor(s). After three failed attempts, the complete OJT shall be repeated."
- AMC: assessment has a theoretical part (regulatory framework, safety procedures, aircraft systems, work order review, shift handover, flight crew interaction, dispatch with unserviceable items, logbook entries, checks before RTS) and a practical part on the aircraft (rem./inst., TS, R/I, FOT, MEL dispatch); training aircraft may be acceptable.

6.4 Mentors and assessors:
- Mentors: valid Part-66 AML (or ICAO Annex 1 compliant AML per Appendix IV to Part-145, acceptable to the authority); "have been holding, for at least 1 year, an AML in the same category ... endorsed with a type rating appropriate to exercise the privileges on the related aircraft"; release/sign-off privileges in the AMO; experience in training others.
- Assessors: valid AML as above; "have been holding, for at least 3 years, an AML in the same category ... endorsed with the same or similar aircraft type rating"; experience/training in assessing; must not have been a mentor of the applicant; if the assessor took part in the OJT, an independent observer must be present.

6.5 OJT documentation: attested by final assessment report plus OJT logbook; provided to the authority with the licence application (Section B, Subpart B); records kept by the AMO where the OJT was conducted per procedures agreed with its authority.

AMC1 Section 6 key numbers and rules:
- OJT may be split across several AMOs, line and base.
- Tasks: use OSD/MASE tasks (CS-MCSD point 430) where they exist; otherwise Appendix II to AMC to Part-66 task list. "A minimum number of tasks, as described in point 2 'List of tasks for OJT' of Appendix II, of each of the following categories should be performed: INS/inspections, FOT/functional or operational, SGH/servicing, R/I removal and installation, MEL, and T/S troubleshooting." (Minimum numbers themselves are in Appendix II to AMC, not in this chunk.)
- Tasks on an analogous system of another type allowed if similar; must be clearly identified and recorded.
- Some tasks on non-airworthy aircraft or in the workshop (e.g. avionics functional tests) allowed in limited cases.
- "The use of MSTDs and MTDs for OJT should be restricted to a minimum."
- Category change with type rating: only difference tasks needed.
- Part may be on non-EASA aircraft (e.g. FAA, military training helicopters) if same procedures and manuals; a minimum on aircraft subject to Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 is required.
- Simulated release to service document marked "for training purposes only" (ATL page, task card, CRS); copy added to the syllabus.
- Group tasks: "the mentor is limited to overseeing three candidates at the same time"; must be marked "group tasks" in the approval; all candidates noted on the work order.
- End-of-OJT compliance report with mentor recommendation; mentors may deny recommendation.
- Independent observer chosen by the AMO from personnel not involved in the OJT.

### 2.5 Appendix III AMC/GM on type training generally

- Course sub-division: airframe, powerplant (bare engine to QEC), avionics/electrical (ATA 22, 23, 24, 25, 27, 31, 33, 34, 42, 44, 45, 46, 73 and 77 or equivalent).
- Practical training must not be performed before theoretical training.
- Content based on type design and variants, in-service feedback, significant ADs and SBs, human factors, documentation (MMEL, AMM, MPD, TSM, SRM, WD, AFM), ETOPS, ALI/CDCCL, CMR, FTS, RVSM, NVIS, environmental factors.
- B1 includes limited avionics training; electrical systems in both B1 and B2.
- MSTD (maintenance simulation training device) and MTD (maintenance training device) usage described; FSTDs may serve as MSTDs.
- Translators: may translate questions only, not clarify.
- Differences training (AMC to 1(c)): not required between variants within the same type rating for endorsement, but the AMO may still require training before certifying authorisation (AMC 66.A.20(b)3).
- GM1 1(c): combined B1+B2 course followed by B1-only OJT gives B1 endorsement only; B2 endorsement possible "Within the next 3 years from the completion of the combined training course" with B2-only OJT; after more than 3 years, a B1-to-B2 differences course plus B2-only OJT is needed.
- AMC1 Section 3: methods are "instructor-centred", "student-centred" and "blended"; complex/critical subjects (identified by TNA, including TCH MASE in OSD) not normally taught solely student-centred.
- AMC1 3.1(d) TNA: the TNA is the main driver of course duration, above or below the minimum; record training time per chapter; reduction below minimum assessed case by case by the authority. "The maximum number of training hours per day for the theoretical element of type training should not be more than 6 hours. A training hour means 60 minutes of tuition excluding any breaks, examination, revision, preparation and aircraft visit." Minimum participation "not less than 90 % of the tuition hours, or 95 % completion of the content in case of student-centred methods"; if not met, no certificate of recognition. TNA is a living process. Pre-1 August 2012 (Regulation (EU) No 1149/2011) courses may run a difference-only TNA.

### 2.9 Appendix IX — MBT assessment

- Competent authority rates each of 20 criteria in Table (A) on a 1–5 scale: 1 Not acceptable; 2 Partially acceptable; 3 Acceptable; 4 Good; 5 Excellent.
- Any criterion below 3: authority requests an alternative learning process.
- Combined score bands: 100–80 Excellent; 79–60 meets criteria; 59–40 "informal" training only; 39–20 below average.
- "Before approving the product, the competent authority shall check that the final score of the MBT is equal to or above 60, and that there is no single criterion that is rated below 3."
- Table (A) categories: (a) academic quality (criteria 1–2: reliability, relevance); (b) pedagogical quality (3–15: formulation/construction, strategies, student self-assessment); (c) didactic quality (16–17: real-life situations, adequate content); (d) technical quality (18–20: design, browsing/navigation, multimedia techniques). Product identification: Name, Version; per-criterion score; final score.

## 4. Defined Terms

- **OJT**: "the training that the applicant is given on a particular aircraft type in a real workplace, having the possibility to learn maintenance best practices and correct release-to-service procedures."
- **Mentor / Assessor / Independent observer**: see 6.4 and AMC.
- **Designated assessors appropriately qualified**: demonstrate training and experience in the assessment process and authorised by the organisation.
- **Maintenance organisations appropriately approved ... for the maintenance of that aircraft type**: Part-145 or Part-CAO AMOs holding an "A" rating for such aircraft.
- **Airframe / Powerplant / Avionics-electrical type training course** (AMC1 Section 1), as in 2.5.
- **MSTD** (maintenance simulation training device): device representing location/access/layout and/or onboard indications with varying simulation, including BIT, troubleshooting. **MTD**: "any training device other than an MSTD used for maintenance training and/or examination and/or assessment." FSTDs may be used as MSTDs. (Defined in AMC 147.A.30(a).)
- **Training methods**: instructor-centred, student-centred, blended.
- **TNA** (training needs analysis); **MASE** (maintenance areas of special/specific emphasis, CS-MCSD point 430); **OSD** (operational suitability data).
- **Training hour**: "60 minutes of tuition excluding any breaks, examination, revision, preparation and aircraft visit."
- **Practical table glossary**: LOC, FOT, SGH, R/I, MEL, TS (and AMC INS for inspections).
- **Group tasks**: tasks with up to three candidates under one mentor.
- **SQ**: "it depends on system qualification" (Appendix IV).
- **L subcategories** L1C, L1, L2C, L2, L3H, L3G, L4H, L4G, L5 (2.7).
- **"Dirty Dozen"**: lack of communication, lack of teamwork, lack of assertiveness, complacency, fatigue, stress, lack of knowledge, lack of resources, lack of awareness, distraction, pressure, norms.
- **Limitations** (Form 26): "exclusions from the certification privileges".
- **MBT**: multimedia-based training.

### 5.2 Training/examination/OJT tracking

- Type training course record: type rating, course parts (airframe, powerplant, avionics), theoretical hours per chapter (TNA), attendance percentage (90 % tuition or 95 % content threshold), max 6 h/day check, theoretical-before-practical ordering, certificate of recognition.
- Type training exam: question count (>= 1 per training hour, multiple of 4), time (90 s/question), score, 75 % pass per exam, multi-part exams, closed book flag.
- Practical training: task checklist from the 3.2 table filtered by aircraft applicability and category (B1/B2), tracking >= 50 % of relevant crossed items, OSD minimum tasks, practical duration (>= 2 weeks for aeroplanes MTOM >= 30 000 kg unless justified), assessor sign-off.
- Type evaluation (Group 2/3): attempts log (max 3 per 12 months; 30-day wait after first failure, 60-day after second), examiner independence check, report, 3-year window to endorsement.
- OJT: programme approval date by licensing authority; eligibility check (A/B/L5 licence, or theory done plus 50 % basic experience); start/complete within 3 years before first type rating application; >= 50 % tasks after theory; per-task fields (description, job card/WO/tech log ref, location, date, aircraft registration, task category INS/FOT/SGH/R/I/MEL/TS, analogous-system flag, group task flag with up to 3 candidates, applicant signature, mentor countersignature); mentors (name, licence number, category, years held >= 1, type rating, training experience); assessor (>= 3 years, not a mentor, observer required if involved); recommendation; final assessment (date notified to authority in advance, duration >= 1 working day, result, signatures); retake rule (3 months, or earlier with additional training plus new recommendation; after 3 failures repeat whole OJT); retention at the AMO.
- B1/B2 combined course differences: 3-year window from course completion for B2 endorsement without a differences course.
- Licence extension calculator: Appendix IV Table A months (with 50 % reduction for Part-147 basic course) and Table B modules required (with SQ, * and ** qualifiers).
- L-licence exam tracker: modules per subcategory matrix, question counts/times, 75 % pass, 90-day retake bar, 3 attempts per 12 months, submodule question distribution for question bank building.
- Form 19 generator: pre-fill applicant, employer (approval reference), application type, category grid, B2L system ratings, type endorsements, credits, declarations, organisation recommendation.
- MBT assessment (mainly for Part-147/authority users): 20 criteria scoring, threshold 60, no criterion < 3.
