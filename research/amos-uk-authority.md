# UK authority evidence for AMOS cases 1–5 and 17–18

This check uses UK CAA primary sources. **Rule** denotes a provision of the applicable UK regulation. **AMC** denotes a CAA acceptable means of compliance for the specified provision. **Guidance**, **industry standard** and **project choice** have their own labels. The roles below are proposed reviewers. Their participation and any authority acceptance remain outstanding.

The product baseline covers in-scope UK Part-21 aircraft under Part-ML and a Part-CAO acting within its actual approval privileges. Article 3(2) applies Part-ML to non-complex aeroplanes up to 2,730 kg, rotorcraft up to 1,200 kg certified for up to four occupants, and other ELA2 aircraft, subject to the licensed-air-carrier AOC exclusion. Part-M applies to that excluded population. A Part-CAO may separately cover qualifying Part-M aircraft subject to its approval and terms. Holding several approvals does not make their procedures interchangeable. [Article 3](https://regulatorylibrary.caa.co.uk/1321-2014/Content/Document%20Structure/00%20Introduction%20and%20Main%20Body/2%20Regs/00050_art._3_Continuing_airworthiness_requirements.htm) · [CAA Part-CAO scope](https://www.caa.co.uk/general-aviation/aircraft-ownership-and-maintenance/part-cao/)

| **1. Faceted maintenance history** | **Rule:** CAO.A.055(b) requires an agreed written order before maintenance that identifies the scope. ML.A.305 sets aircraft record contents and clear, traceable corrections. ML.A.403 governs defect control. CAO.A.090 covers records held by the CAO. For Part-M aircraft map M.A.305 and M.A.403. A Part-145 record set follows 145.A.55 with the applicable Part-M or Part-ML record route. **Industry standard:** ATA iSpec 2200 numbering. **Project choice:** chapter/section semantics and combined facets, separate search fields, source links, keyboard operation and tenancy controls. | Continuing airworthiness planner and technical records lead to confirm retrieval semantics and that a search result preserves the separate work-order and defect roles. |
| **2. Structural damage and repair register** | **Rule:** ML.A.304 requires assessing damage and using applicable CAA-approved, Part-21 design-organisation-approved, or qualifying Part-21 standard repair data. ML.A.305(d)(2) and (h)(6) cover current repair status and retention of repair details. CAO.A.055(a) covers applicable current data and CAO.A.090(a)(1) requires supplying the owner a copy of specific repair data used. Where Part-M applies, map M.A.304/M.A.305. **AMC:** AMC M.A.304 addresses assessment against published approved data and escalation for damage outside it. **Industry standard:** ATA Spec 2500 is a transfer mapping reference. **Project choice:** charts, marker links, revision history and synthetic lightning-strike exercise. The record should identify the approval or standard-repair basis, exact source revision and applicable effectivity. | Structures engineer and technical records lead. Actual aircraft operation needs access and rights to the applicable data from the approval holder or design data owner and confirmation of the repair basis and any recurring inspection. |
| **3. Per-flight log and provenance** | **Rule:** ML.A.403(d) requires a defect unrectified before flight to be recorded in ML.A.305 records and available to the pilot. ML.A.403(b) separately controls deferral authority. Part-M M.A.306 requires an operator ATL for CAT, commercial specialised operations and commercial ATO/DTO, with CAA approval of initial issue. Subsequent amendments follow CAMO.A.300(c), M.A.704(b)/(c) or CAO.A.025(c), as applicable. M.B.305(b) sets the approved procedure route for amendment without prior approval. **AMC:** AMC M.A.306(a) gives ATL contents including flight details and defects known to the commander. **Industry standard:** ATA Spec 2000 chapter 17. **Project choice:** per-flight feature within the supported Part-ML slice, structured part linkage, separately attributed reporter, recorder and certifier, and authorised transcription. | Flight crew representative and maintenance control. The operator owns the approved reporting/transcription procedure and, if deploying an ATL required by M.A.306, the relevant approval/amendment evidence. |
| **4. Stock location and eligibility** | **Rule:** CAO.A.030(b) requires secure and segregated storage, compliance with manufacturer's storage instructions and access for authorised personnel. CAO.A.050(c) requires inspection, classification and segregation of incoming components under ML.A.501/504 or M.A.501/504 as applicable. ML.A.501 addresses serviceability, release and marking with specified exceptions and eligibility for AD/modification configuration. Part-145 separately applies 145.A.42 acceptance, installation eligibility from applicable maintenance data and segregation. **AMC:** AMC1 CAO.A.030 supplements storage. **Industry standard:** ATA Spec 2000 chapter 9 is a traceability reference. **Project choice:** custody and availability counts, reservations, expiry/quarantine gates at issue, retries, concurrency and training checkpoints. Actual alternatives need applicable maintenance data and technical eligibility. | Stores inspector and maintenance engineer. The approval holder and data owner must identify current eligible alternatives and valid release/traceability evidence for live inventory. |
| **5. Intraday dispatch and handover** | **Rule:** CAO.A.035(d) requires sufficient appropriately qualified staff for planned work. CAO.A.060 requires qualified performers and reducing multiple or repeated maintenance errors. The statutory shift planning, human performance/fatigue and handover clauses are 145.A.47(a)–(c) for Part-145. **AMC:** AMC 145.A.47(c) elaborates effective handover. **Research context:** NASA shift-turnover study. **Project choice:** applying similar guards to CAO planning, break/overlap warnings, accessible alternative and incoming acknowledgement. | Line maintenance planner and human factors specialist. Real operation requires approved local procedures and validation of warnings, capacity data and handover responsibilities by the operator. |
| **17. Management of change and parallel operation** | **Rule:** CAO.A.105(a) requires pre-notification for enumerated triggers including approval terms and changes to the CAE change-control procedure. CAA's Part-CAO guidance describes the applicable approval or acceptance route. Other changes follow the CAE procedure and CAO.A.105(b), with description and amendments submitted within 15 days of change. CAO.A.100 governs quality system or permitted organisational review. Part-145 uses 145.A.85 and .200, Part-CAMO uses CAMO.A.130 and .200. An initial required Part-M ATL follows M.A.306/M.B.305. **AMC:** AMC1 145.A.200(a)(3) and AMC1 CAMO.A.200(a)(3) address safety risk management including change. **Guidance:** CAP 562 C-90 pages 1–3, especially §§6.3–6.5, describes a normal six-month hard-copy parallel period, a problem log and exposition incorporation for the context discussed. The leaflet cites the ANO 2009. Its scope and any duration for a specific modern deployment must be established with the approval holder and CAA. **Project choice:** bounded synthetic dual-role rehearsal, authoritative record, discrepancy log, escalation and fallback. | Both organisations' accountable leads, compliance/quality leads and working users. A designated approval holder owns change classification and the real parallel protocol. Its CAA surveyor or competent authority owns any applicable acceptance or prior approval. |
| **18. Readiness, cutover and stabilisation** | **Rule:** use the applicable case 17 change route and preserve aircraft and organisational records obligations throughout. Where the operational event is an actual aircraft or management transfer, ML.A.307 provides the transfer rule for Part-ML, with M.A.307 used where Part-M applies. CAO.A.090(d) requires storage protected from damage, alteration and theft. **Guidance:** CAP 562 C-90 informs training, recovery and reconciliation. **Project choice:** dated readiness evidence, go/no-go and stop thresholds, rollback drills, open-item ownership and stabilisation. The Swiss-AS schedule is historical context. | Project sponsor, operational lead, training lead, technical records lead and receiver. The relevant accountable manager or designated operational decision-maker owns a recorded production go/no-go, supported by actual reconciliation and training and applicable regulator evidence. |
