# ADR 0005. Staffing plan records

27 November 2026

The application records the monthly staffing plan under CAO.A.035(d), CAMO.A.305(d) and 145.A.30(d), together with reviews, absences and evidence of a report under AMC1 145.A.30(d). This extends the documented function through REQ-324 to REQ-327.

Each entry identifies the approval, month, function and the basis of the required and available hours. A report snapshots the inputs used when it was recorded. Its history remains available after updating the plan. The reporting comparison uses the unrounded shortage. The displayed percentage is rounded to the nearest whole number.
