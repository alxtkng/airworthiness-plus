# ADR-0009. Tool register and control evidence

Status: Proposed for review, 28 November 2026
Applies to REQ-360 to REQ-363 and Logistics issue #19.

The register adds a regulated function under UK CAO.A.050(b), CAO.A.090 and 145.A.40(b). It records the organisation's tooling controls and displays label data for the selected maintenance approval. An organisation adopting it describes the actual delegated powers, evidence procedures and physical labels in its CAE or MOE.

## Records and decisions

- One physical asset belongs to one organisation. A case-insensitive asset ID is unique there. A maintenance approval changes the applicable control view. It leaves the asset identity and any physical restriction shared.
- Each approval can have a concurrent calibration, servicing and before-each-use requirement. A revision retains its reference and revision, scope, method, acceptance limits, recognised calibration standard where applicable, and rationale. Control selection uses the current approval reference and the latest effective revision of each kind.
- A completion names the applicable revision and supplies a retrievable evidence reference. The authorised signer adopts the completion under the current procedure. A changed basis needs an explicit decision to rely on any earlier completion. A pre-use observation leaves the before-each-use requirement in place. A passing interim check leaves the calibration deadline unchanged.
- A physical restriction stays in force until a delegated person signs return to use after completed rectification and checks the applicable controls. Return to use takes effect on the date of signature. A confirmed withdrawal stays in the register history. Held condition and dates stay visible together.
- The Administrator transcribes the signed exposition delegation and any withdrawal. The technical signer needs a linked, active person record, the application action, and a live grant covering the action, site and approval reference. Validity is inclusive at both ends and a recorded withdrawal applies immediately. The application enforces the named site and action. The signer checks any narrower scope or competency conditions in the cited delegation. The scope of an actual customer's CAE or MOE remains for that organisation to establish.
- The calendar convention is a local product decision. Add the whole stated interval to the accepted completion date using the corresponding calendar day or the last day of the shorter target month. A later completion gets its own anniversary. The due date remains current until the next UK local date. The organisation's lead time controls the reminder. A missing basis or completion leaves the deadline blank. Where a controlling instruction requires a different convention, verify that the supported basis is applicable before using the label.
- Append-only PostgreSQL records enforce organisation scope, parent consistency and audited actor attribution. An organisation row lock serialises competing decisions and submission retries. Keep all five record types in organisation export. Aircraft exports gain work-linked evidence as the use and reconciliation workflow is built.

## Regulatory and technical sources

The UK CAA Regulatory Library gives the rule and AMC.
