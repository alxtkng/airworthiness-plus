# ADR-0010. Calibration custody and certificate evidence

Status: Proposed for review, 28 November 2026

REQ-364 to REQ-369 add provider scope, acceptance and calibration records under UK CAO.A.050(b), CAO.A.090 and 145.A.40(b). The organisation's CAE or MOE governs which people may authorise a provider, order work, record custody, enter results, accept a certificate and reinstate a tool. The application's roles give access to the forms. The separate dated delegation and approval reference govern the technical act. Confirmation of actual competence and any narrower procedure conditions stays with the signer.

- A supplier keeps its existing identity. Authorisation identifies the service and provider facility, measurement type, method revision, unit and range, under a maintenance site and approval. Later scope or suspension evidence supersedes the same approval, site, facility, measurement, method and unit from its effective date. A new calibration order needs eligible calibration scope at placement. Despatch and technical acceptance also check scope for their respective event dates. Part-145 authorisation requires a reference to the relevant accreditation scope schedule under this application policy.
- Orders specify the direction/configuration, required reference points, unit, maximum absolute signed error, applicable limits source and revision, and any required uncertainty or decision rule. The order borrows the recognised standard from the pinned control revision. Despatch, expected return, actual return and certificate receipt each have a distinct meaning. The prior reported label may be recorded with a source when the old deadline cannot be reconstructed. It supplies no calibration completion.
- Certificate versions retain the reported identity and raw results even when they warrant rejection. The service checks exact serial and controlled component identities, applicable coverage, reference points, provider facility, method, scope dates, required uncertainty, standard and traceability before acceptance. A declared conformity statement requires its cited decision rule. When using measurements for an internal conformity decision, the application supports a documented whole expanded-uncertainty interval rule. The numeric comparison assumes the reported signed error, uncertainty and maximum error share the ordered unit. The operator supplies the actual error convention and decision-rule evidence. A missing or inconclusive as-found result is visible for triage. For this workflow the order specifies one direction/configuration and a symmetric error limit.
- PostgreSQL creates the case with the result insert and enforces one original trigger per order. A stated nonconformity or a signed numerical error beyond the ordered limit opens the case. Inclusive comparison keeps exactly ± the ordered limit at its numerical boundary. Incomplete evidence and findings reported only as-left are provisional triggers pending investigation. The result fixes the relevant original evidence and actor. The case creates a calibration hold independently of certificate acceptance. Despatch through pending reinstatement also holds the tool. A corrected certificate preserves previous results and decisions and requires current review.
- Reinstatement separately requires physical return, current acceptance, the applicable control revision, unexpired calibration, the other applicable controls and any applicable rectification. The signer records procedure-required interim action evidence. The due date is anchored to the calibration date under ADR-0009. A signed interval-only revision may support the label when the technical scope and applicable instructions still match the accepted certificate. An earlier completion cannot support readiness after an adverse finding without refreshed or reaccepted evidence under the applicable approval. Reinstatement affects future use for the covered scope and leaves the affected-work case open. PR 4 implements investigation and closure.
