# ADR 0006. Production planning records

27 November 2026

REQ-328 to REQ-331 extend the system into production planning, roster, written handovers and acknowledgement under 145.A.47, and the Part-145 maintenance crew calculation under AMC1 145.A.30(d). Organisation-defined operational settings also serve any approval for which the organisation uses them.

Roster time is entered as UK local time. Elapsed time accounts for clock changes. Work packages contribute work and planned inspection hours once to the week in which they start. Maintenance roster hours supply availability, excluding shifts conflicting with an absence. The board uses the underlying due calculation. Settings, shift withdrawals, publications and handovers are retained in the record history.
